# Phase 3 Global Audit — Iteration 01: The 4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Standard Across Chapters 01–24

## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Scope

- **Audit Pass**: Phase 3, Iteration 01 (`phase3_iter01_zero_magic_4step_compilation.md`)
- **Target Corpus**: All 24 Chapters (`flows/chapters/ch01-*.qmd` through `flows/chapters/ch24-*.qmd`), 6 Blind Checkpoints (`flows/checkpoints/cp01-*.qmd` through `flows/checkpoints/cp06-*.qmd`), and 2 Capstones (`flows/capstones/synthesis-reserve.qmd`, `flows/capstones/problem-archive-guide.qmd`).
- **Core Mandate Audited**: In accordance with `AGENTS.md` and `flows_research/PLAN.md` Section 2.1, **zero unmotivated graph constructions ("magic gadgets") are permitted anywhere in the textbook**. Every modelling paradigm and every solved problem (`#001`–`#256`) must walk through the explicit **4-Step Compilation Protocol**:
  1. **Step 1 — Non-Graph Diagnostic Signal (Recognition)**: What surface English/algebraic features and constraint scales ($N, M, U$) trigger the flow/cut/matching/MCMF/duality hypothesis, and why do naive DP or greedy approaches fail?
  2. **Step 2 — Intermediate Algebraic / Logical Representation**: How is the English problem translated into a mathematical intermediate representation *before* drawing any vertex or edge (e.g., conservation equations, pseudo-Boolean cut polynomials $E(\mathbf{x}) = \sum c_{uv} x_u (1 - x_v)$, unary threshold indicators $z_{i,k} = [x_i \ge k]$, consecutive equation differences $\Delta E_t = E_t - E_{t-1}$, or LP dual variables)?
  3. **Step 3 — Reverse-Engineering Scratchpad (Mechanical Graph Compilation)**: How does each algebraic term in Step 2 compile mechanically into vertices, directed arcs, capacities $[l_e, u_e]$, and unit costs $w_e$?
  4. **Step 4 — Hand-Checkable Toy Trace & Broken-Gadget Counterexample (`FM-01`–`FM-26`)**: A concrete numerical trace on a small hand-checkable instance plus an explicit demonstration of what breaks when a structural invariant is violated.
