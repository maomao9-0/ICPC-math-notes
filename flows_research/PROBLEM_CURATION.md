#### Chapter 01: Conservation Laws, Skew-Symmetry, Residual Graphs & The Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem (`ch01_conservation_residual_maxflow_mincut.qmd`)
- **Polyhedral / Operator Scope**: Flow conservation $\nabla \cdot f(v) = 0$, skew-symmetry $f(u,v) = -f(v,u)$, residual capacity $c_f(u,v) = c(u,v) - f(u,v)$, $s$-$t$ cut function $c(S,T)$ $\mid$ **Prereqs**: None (Entry Point; seals Dinic behind black-box contract) $\mid$ **Waves**: `1A (§1–§2, §5)`
- **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  - **1.1** Conservation Laws, Skew-Symmetry & Residual Graphs: Why backward arcs $v \to u$ with residual capacity $f(u,v)$ encode lossless flow rerouting (`Cancellation ≠ Deletion`).
  - **1.2** Augmenting Paths, Ford–Fulkerson & The Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem: Equivalence of (i) maximum flow $f^*$, (ii) no $s \leadsto t$ path in $G_{f^*}$, and (iii) $S = \{v : s \leadsto_{G_{f^*}} v\}$ achieving $|f^*| = c(S, T)$; Integrality Theorem.
  - **1.3** Extracting Minimum Cuts & All-Destination Enumeration: Recovering $S_{\min}$ via residual BFS from $s$; resetting vs. incrementally updating residual graphs across destination queries.
  - **1.4** Dinic's Blocking-Flow Complexity Invariants & Unit-Network Regimes: $O(V^2 E)$ general, $O(E\sqrt{V})$ unit-capacity simple networks, and $O(V^{2/3}E)$ unit-capacity multigraphs; Prime-parity bipartite layer separation.
- **Diagnostic Signal & Scratchpad**: Throughput allocation, sports elimination feasibility ("can team 1 still finish with the most wins?"), binary search on bottleneck rate $w$ with $\lfloor c_e/w \rfloor$ integer capacities, or $K \le 10$ dynamic edges with $Q \le 2\times 10^5$ queries. $\mid$ *Scratchpad*: 1. Identify conserved quantities (points, bears, soldiers, parity pairs). 2. Wire sources of supply to $S$ and capacity ceilings to $T$. 3. Check whether feasibility equals full saturation of all source/sink arcs.
- **Flagship Worked Example (`CF 1383F` — Special Edges)**: *Problem*: Given a directed network ($N, M \le 10^4, c_e \le 25$) where $K \le 10$ special edges receive new capacities in each of $Q \le 2\times 10^5$ queries, output the max-flow per query. *Derivation*: Set the $K$ special edges to capacity $0$ and compute a base max-flow $F_0$. Across all $2^K$ subsets of special edges, their residual cut capacities $R[\text{mask}]$ can be precomputed in Gray-code order by cloning the residual graph and augmenting a single capacity-25 edge in $O(25 \cdot M)$ per step; each query then takes $O(2^K)$ via $\min_{\text{mask}}(R[\text{mask}] + \sum_{i \notin \text{mask}} w_i)$.

#### Chapter 02: Residual Graph Anatomy, Essential/Possible Cut Edges & Dynamic Rollback (`ch02_residual_anatomy_cut_classification_rollback.qmd`)
- **Polyhedral / Operator Scope**: Residual reachability poset $G_{f^*}$, canonical extremal cuts $S_{\min}$ and $S_{\max}$, Tarjan SCC quotient of $G_{f^*}$, surgical flow rollback (`退流`) $\mid$ **Prereqs**: Ch 01 $\mid$ **Waves**: `1A (§2–§3), 1B (§3)`
- **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  - **2.1** Extremal Minimum Cuts ($S_{\min}, S_{\max}$), Lexicographic Perturbation & 2-Commodity Swap: Why $c'_e = c_e(M+1) + 1$ minimizes cut cardinality; why testing both $(a_1 \to a_2, b_1 \to b_2)$ and $(a_1 \to a_2, b_2 \to b_1)$ eliminates cross-commodity interference (`[CQOI2014] 危桥`).
  - **2.2** `[AHOI2009] 最小割` Theorem — Classifying Essential & Possible Cut Edges via Tarjan SCC on $G_{f^*}$: Saturated edge $(u,v)$ is in *some* minimum cut iff $\operatorname{scc}(u) \neq \operatorname{scc}(v)$ in $G_{f^*}$, and in *every* minimum cut iff $\operatorname{scc}(u) = \operatorname{scc}(s) \land \operatorname{scc}(v) = \operatorname{scc}(t)$.
  - **2.3** Incremental Augmentation, Offline Time-Reversal & Surgical Flow Rollback (`退流`): Deleting a saturated edge $(u,v)$ of flow $f_e$ without recomputing from scratch by pushing $f_e$ units along $u \leadsto s$ and $t \leadsto v$ (or running $\text{MaxFlow}(u, s)$ and $\text{MaxFlow}(t, v)$).
  - **2.4** Constructive Preflow Conservation & Greedy Orientation Certificates: Orienting undirected networks when prefix inflows hit half-degree thresholds (`CF 269C`).
- **Diagnostic Signal & Scratchpad**: Questions asking "which edges belong to *at least one* / *every* minimum cut?", "find the lexicographically smallest minimum cut," "delete vertices/edges online," or "route two commodities across an undirected network." $\mid$ *Scratchpad*: 1. #### Chapter 11: Bipartite Variable Inversion (Parity Flip) & Rescuing Supermodular Costs (`ch11_bipartite_variable_inversion_supermodular_cuts.qmd`)
- **Polyhedral / Operator Scope**: Supermodular pairwise cost $\psi(0,0) + \psi(1,1) > \psi(0,1) + \psi(1,0)$, Bipartite Variable Inversion $y_v = 1 - x_v$ for $v \in R$, $K$-state reversed threshold chain $w_{v,t} = 1 - z_{v,K-t}$ $\mid$ **Prereqs**: Ch 10 $\mid$ **Waves**: `2A (§4–§5), 2B (§2)`
- **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  - **11.1** Why Supermodular Costs ($A + D > B + C$) Fail on General Graphs (`FM-11`: NP-Hard Max-Cut) & How Bipartite Variable Inversion Rescues Them: When the conflict graph $G = (L \sqcup R, E)$ is bipartite, substituting $y_v = 1 - x_v$ for $v \in R$ swaps columns $0 \leftrightarrow 1$ of every pairwise matrix $\psi_{uv}$, turning $\psi(0,0) + \psi(1,1) > \psi(0,1) + \psi(1,0)$ into a submodular cut polynomial $\tilde{\psi}(0,0) + \tilde{\psi}(1,1) < \tilde{\psi}(0,1) + \tilde{\psi}(1,0)$! Generalization to $K$-state chain reversal (`yukicoder 1541`).
  - **11.2** Chessboard Parity Inversion & L-Omino / Brick Conflict Graphs: Flipping $y_{r,c} = 1 - x_{r,c}$ on odd $(r+c) \bmod 2$ cells to turn monochrome penalties $[x_u = x_v]$ (`ABC 193F — Zebraness`) or mutual-difference rewards $+C_{uv}[x_u \neq x_v]$ (`[国家集训队] 圈地计划`) into standard $s$-$t$ cuts; horizontal-vs-vertical cell-merge conflict in `CF 1404E — Bricks`.
  - **11.3** Row-vs-Column & Task-vs-Machine Bipartite Inversions: Inverting column variables $y_c = 1 - x_c$ so simultaneous row-and-column selection $(x_r = 1, x_c = 1)$ becomes the cut event $(x_r = 1 \in S, y_c = 0 \in T)$ (`ABC 259G — Grid Card Game`, `CEOI 2008 Order`).
  - **11.4** User Benchmark Flagship Derivation — `AtCoder AGC 038 F` (`Two Permutations`): Decomposing $P$ and $Q$ into disjoint cycles where every cycle $C \in \mathcal{C}_P$ chooses $x_C \in \{0 \text{ (keep)}, 1 \text{ (rotate)}\}$ and every cycle $D \in \mathcal{C}_Q$ chooses $z_D \in \{0 \text{ (keep)}, 1 \text{ (rotate)}\}$; inverting $y_D = 1 - z_D$ so all 5 per-index cases ($P_i = i, Q_i = i, P_i = Q_i$, etc.) compile into submodular $C \leftrightarrow D$ cut edges!
- **Diagnostic Signal & Scratchpad**: Penalties when two adjacent/coupled choices are **equal** ($[x_u = x_v]$), rewards when they **differ** ($[x_u \neq x_v]$), or penalties when a row and column are **simultaneously chosen** ($x_r x_c$). $\mid$ *Scratchpad*: 1. - **Flagship Worked Example (`AtCoder AGC 038F` — Two Permutations, `#087`)**: *Problem*: Given two permutations $P, Q$ of $\{0..N-1\}$ ($N \le 10^5$), construct permutations $A, B$ where $A_i \in \{i, P_i\}$ and $B_i \in \{i, Q_i\}$ maximizing $|\{i : A_i \neq B_i\}|$. *Derivation*: In any valid permutation $A$, each cycle $C$ of $P$ must either fix all its elements ($A_i = i, x_C = 0$) or shift all its elements ($A_i = P_i, x_C = 1$). For each index $i$ in cycle $C \in \mathcal{C}_P$ and $D \in \mathcal{C}_Q$, the condition $A_i = B_i$ can require $x_C = 0 \land z_D = 0$ (always if $P_i \neq i, Q_i \neq i, P_i \neq Q_i$) or $x_C = 1 \land z_D = 1$ (if $P_i = Q_i \neq i$). Both $x_C = z_D = 0$ and $x_C = z_D = 1$ are **supermodular** agreement penalties! Invert $y_D = 1 - z_D$ on $Q$-cycles: then $x_C = z_D = 0 \iff (x_C = 0, y_D = 1)$ compiles to $D \xrightarrow{1} C$, and $x_C = z_D = 1 \iff (x_C = 1, y_D = 0)$ compiles to $C \xrightarrow{1} D$, solved in $O(N\sqrt{N})$ via Dinic!

  - **18.2** Coupled Two-Variable Convex Costs (`[JSOI2009] 球队收益`): Eliminating $l_i$ from $C_i w_i^2 + D_i l_i^2$ via $w_i + l_i = \text{total}_i$: pre-pay the all-loss baseline so every additional win has strictly increasing marginal cost $(C_i + D_i)(2w - 1) - 2D_i \text{rem}_i$.
  - **18.3** Landau's Tournament 3-Cycle Complement (`[WC2007] 剪刀石头布`, `CF 1264E`): Three teams $\{a,b,c\}$ fail to form a 3-cycle iff one team beats the other two ($\binom{w_i}{2}$ non-cyclic triples). Maximizing 3-cycles $\iff \min \sum_i \binom{w_i}{2}$, compiled into parallel out-degree edges of marginal costs $\binom{k}{2} - \binom{k-1}{2} = k-1 \in \{0, 1, \dots, N-2\}$.
  - **18.4** Congestion-Penalized Time-Expanded DAGs (`CF 1187G`): Splitting each street traversal at time $t$ into $K$ parallel unit arcs of cost $c + d(2k-1)$.
- **Diagnostic Signal & Scratchpad**: Costs proportional to $f^2$, $\binom{f}{2}$, piecewise-linear convex tax brackets, coupled win/loss squares $C w^2 + D l^2$, maximizing 3-cycles in a tournament, or maximum Manhattan matching. $\mid$ *Scratchpad*: 1. Compute $\Delta_k = \Phi(k) - \Phi(k-1)$ for $k = 1 \dots U$ and verify $\Delta_1 \le \Delta_2 \le \dots \le \Delta_U$. 2. Add $U$ parallel edges of capacity $1$ and cost $\Delta_k$ (or bundle equal-slope segments into capacity $T_p - T_{p-1}$).

#### Chapter 19: Consecutive Equation Differencing (`差分建图`) & Interval / Tree Flow Compilation (`ch19_consecutive_equation_differencing_interval_flows.qmd`)
- **Polyhedral / Operator Scope**: Consecutive-ones linear system $A x - s = d$, difference operator $\Delta E_i = E_i - E_{i-1}$ ($i = 1 \dots N+1$), tree divergence operator $\Delta E_u = E_u - \sum_{v \in \text{ch}(u)} E_v$, two-sided bounded slack $s_i \in [0, U_i - L_i]$ $\mid$ **Prereqs**: Ch 17 $\mid$ **Waves**: `4A (§3), 4B (§2–§3)`
- **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  - **19.1** Maximum-Weight $k$-Overlap Interval Packing & Coordinate Doubling (`FM-16b`): Routing $k$ units along timeline spine $1 \xrightarrow{(k,0)} 2 \dots \xrightarrow{(k,0)} M$ where interval $[l_j, r_j)$ is a detour arc $l_j \xrightarrow{(1, -w_j)} r_j$ (`Luogu P3358`, `CF 717G`); why vertical segments $x_1 = x_2$ in `Luogu P3357` (`最长k可重线段集问题`) collapse to zero-length self-loops unless coordinates are doubled via $x \mapsto (2x, 2x+1)$!
  - **19.2** Masao Iri's Consecutive Equation Differencing (`差分建图`, `[NOI2008] 志愿者招募`): Given $\sum_{j : i \in [l_j, r_j]} x_j - s_i = A_i$ ($x_j, s_i \ge 0$), taking $\Delta E_i = E_i - E_{i-1}$ ($E_0 \equiv 0, E_{N+1} \equiv 0$) makes every variable $x_j$ appear **exactly twice** ($+x_j$ in $\Delta E_{l_j}$ and $-x_j$ in $\Delta E_{r_j+1}$) and every surplus $s_i$ appear twice ($-s_i$ in $\Delta E_i$ and $+s_i$ in $\Delta E_{i+1}$). Thus $\Delta E_i$ is exact flow conservation at vertex $i$ with interval arc $l_j \xrightarrow{(\infty, c_j)} r_j+1$ and slack arc $i+1 \xrightarrow{(\infty, 0)} i$!
  - **19.3** Two-Sided Window Bounds (`NEERC 2016 Delight for a Cat`): When every sliding window $W_i$ requires $L_i \le \sum_{t \in W_i} x_t \le U_i$, write $\sum_{t \in W_i} x_t - s_i = L_i$ with **bounded slack** $0 \le s_i \le U_i - L_i$, compiling to $i+1 \xrightarrow{(U_i - L_i, 0)} i$!
  - **19.4** Rooted Tree Equation Differencing (`树上差分建图`): When each variable $x_j$ covers a vertical tree path $w_j \leadsto u_j$ (where $u_j$ is an ancestor of $w_j$), subtracting child equations $\Delta E_u = E_u - \sum_{v \in \text{ch}(u)} E_v$ gives $+x_j$ at $w_j$ and $-x_j$ at $\operatorname{parent}(u_j)$, compiling directly into $w_j \xrightarrow{(\infty, c_j)} \operatorname{parent}(u_j)$!
- **Diagnostic Signal & Scratchpad**: Selecting intervals $[l_j, r_j]$ or sliding-window items so that every point/window $i$ has total coverage $\ge A_i$ (or $\in [L_i, U_i]$) at minimum cost. $\mid$ *Scratchpad*: 1. Write the exact equality $E_i$ for each time step $i = 1 \dots N$ using explicit slack variables $s_i \in [0, U_i - L_i]$. 2. Pad $E_0 : 0 = 0$ and $E_{N+1} : 0 = 0$, and compute $\Delta E_i = E_i - E_{i-1}$. 3. Read off every $+z$ in $\Delta E_u$ and $-z$ in $\Delta E_v$ as a directed arc $u \to v$, and every RHS constant $A_i - A_{i-1}$ as vertex balance $M(i)$.
- **Flagship Worked Example (`Luogu P3980` — `[NOI2008]` 志愿者招募 `#149` & `Luogu P6967` — Delight for a Cat `#151`)**: *Problem (`P3980`)*: Day $i \in \{1..N\}$ requires $\ge A_i$ volunteers; type-$j$ volunteers work across $[l_j, r_j]$ at cost $c_j$ per person. Minimize total cost. *Derivation*: Introduce surplus $s_i \ge 0$ so $E_i : \sum_{j : l_j \le i \le r_j} x_j - s_i = A_i$. Subtracting $E_{i-1}$ from $E_i$ ($i = 1 \dots N+1$, with $A_0 = A_{N+1} = 0$) yields:
  $$\sum_{j : l_j = i} x_j - \sum_{j : r_j + 1 = i} x_j + s_{i-1} - s_i = A_i - A_{i-1}.$$
  Viewing `Outflow - Inflow = Demand` at vertex $i \in \{1..N+1\}$: each volunteer type $j$ is a directed edge $l_j \xrightarrow{(\infty, c_j)} r_j + 1$, each surplus $s_i$ is a backward edge $i+1 \xrightarrow{(\infty, 0)} i$, and RHS $b_i = A_i - A_{i-1}$ is wired to $S$ ($b_i > 0$) or $T$ ($b_i < 0$).

#### Chapter 21: Linear Programming Duality, Totally Unimodular Matrices & Dualizing Covering/Potential LPs (`ch21_lp_duality_total_unimodularity_inverse_mst.qmd`)
