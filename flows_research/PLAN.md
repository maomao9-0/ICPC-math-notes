## 1. Goal Description & Core Pedagogical Shift (Why Flows Require a Modelling-First Architecture)

This project creates a comprehensive competitive programming mathematics and algorithmics textbook (`/home/maomao90/Documents/IO/ICPC-math-notes/flows/`) and research corpus (`/home/maomao90/Documents/IO/ICPC-math-notes/flows_research/`) on **Maximum Flow, Minimum Cut, Bipartite & General Graph Matching, Min-Cost Max-Flow (MCMF), Linear Programming Duality, Simulated Cost Flows, and Non-Graph-to-Flow Modelling**, built to the exact same standard of depth, multi-agent redundancy, and mathematical rigor as `fps_research/`.

### The Fundamental Difference Between FPS and Flows

In Formal Power Series (FPS), complexity is split evenly between a large catalog of algebraic/analytic algorithms and combinatorial modelling. In **Network Flows**, by contrast:

1. **The Algorithmic Engine is Compact**: A competitive programmer's library needs only a handful of black-box solvers (Dinic's algorithm, Min-Cost Flow via Successive Shortest Path with Johnson potentials or Primal–Dual, Kuhn–Munkres / Blossom, and Gomory–Hu / Stoer–Wagner). Per `AGENTS.md`, standard solver templates are assumed to be in the reader's library and are never dumped as boilerplate code.
2. **90%+ of the Difficulty is Recognition and Graph Construction (Modelling)**:
   - **Unintuitive Recognition ("Why is this a flow problem at all?")**: The hardest flow problems—such as `ARC 107 F — Sum of Abs`, `ARC 142 E — Pairing Wizards`, `CF 1427 G — One Billion Shades of Grey`, and `AGC 038 F — Two Permutations`—often **do not mention graphs, flows, or matchings anywhere in the problem statement**. They are phrased in terms of arrays, permutations, grid cells, absolute value sums, $\max/\min$ inequalities, intervals, strings, games, or algebraic operations.
   - **Unmotivated Gadget Construction ("How on earth did the editorial come up with that graph?")**: Standard editorials notoriously write *"Build a graph with source $S$, sink $T$, split each vertex into $v_{\text{in}}, v_{\text{out}}$, add an edge of capacity $\infty$ from $u_{\text{in}}$ to $v_{\text{out}}$, and the answer is $\sum B_i - \text{MinCut}$"*—treating the graph construction as a "magic trick" without explaining how to systematically derive the vertices, orientations, capacities, and costs from the problem's equations, or why a slightly different edge orientation fails.

## 2. Key Design Decisions & Editorial Standards

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **1. The "Zero Magic Gadgets" Mandate (4-Step English-to-Graph Compilation)**
> The textbook and research files must **never** state an unmotivated graph construction ("Add an edge $u \to v$ with capacity $\infty$..."). Every modelling technique and every worked/practice problem solution must explicitly walk through four steps:
> 1. **The Non-Graph Diagnostic Signal (Recognition)**: What exact feature in the English statement and constraints ($N \le 50$, $V_{\max} \le 100$, $\lvert x_u - x_v \rvert$, $\max(a_x, a_y) \ge c$, "at most $k$ overlapping intervals", reversible choices, $2 \times 2$ payoff matrices, or $N \le 10^5$ with convex/tree/interval structure) tells you to think of Flow, Min-Cut, Matching, MCMF, LP Duality, or Simulated Flow? Why do standard DP and greedy fail (e.g., cyclic dependencies, high treewidth, global capacity coupling)?
> 2. **The Algebraic / Logical Intermediate Representation**: Before drawing any graph, translate the English statement into a clean mathematical intermediate model:
>    - *For Min-Cut (`燃やす埋める` / Project Selection / Multi-State Cuts)*: Define binary indicator variables $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$ (or unary threshold chains $z_{i,t} = [a_i \ge t]$ with $z_{i,1} \ge z_{i,2} \ge \dots$) and write the objective as a **Pseudo-Boolean Cut Polynomial** $\sum c_{u,v} x_u(1 - x_v)$ plus hard implication constraints $x_u = 1 \implies x_v = 1$ ($x_u (1 - x_v) = 0$).
>    - *For Flow Conservation / MCMF (`流守恒方程`)*: Write the linear inequality system, add non-negative slack variables, and take **Consecutive Equation Differences** ($E_t - E_{t-1}$) or the **Linear Programming Dual** so every variable appears once with $+1$ (out-flow) and once with $-1$ (in-flow).
>    - *For Coarea / Level-Set Decomposition (`CF 1427G` / Isotonic Regression)*: Rewrite $\ell_1$ or convex differences $\sum w_{uv} |h_u - h_v|$ via the discrete Coarea Formula $\sum_{\lambda} (\lambda_{k+1} - \lambda_k) \sum w_{uv} \big|[h_u > \lambda_k] - [h_v > \lambda_k]\big|$ as decoupled binary threshold cuts.
> 3. **The Reverse-Engineering Scratchpad (Graph Compilation)**: Show how each term in the intermediate algebraic formula mechanically compiles into a specific vertex, directed edge, capacity, lower bound, or cost—including how to rescue **supermodular** $2 \times 2$ cost matrices ($A + D > B + C$) via **Bipartite Variable Inversion** ($x_j \gets 1 - x_j$ on one side of a bipartite graph, as in `AGC 038 F` and `ARC 142 E`).
> 4. **Broken Gadget Counterexamples & Failure Modes**: Explicitly demonstrate what breaks if a subtle edge is omitted or misoriented (e.g., omitting reverse $\infty$-edges $u_{i,k} \to u_{i,k-1}$ in a multi-state chain cut allows a min-cut to sever multiple edges of the same chain; applying bipartite inversion on a non-bipartite odd cycle hits NP-hard Max-Cut; ignoring unreachable positive-weight cycles in Project Selection; negative-cost cycles breaking Dijkstra potentials in MCMF).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **2. Bidirectional Duality: "English $\to$ Flow Graph" AND "Flow Graph $\to$ Non-Flow Fast Algorithm"**
> - **Direction A (Non-Graph $\to$ Flow)**: Translating a problem with no graphs at all (`ARC 107 F`, `ARC 142 E`, `AGC 038 F`, `NOI2008 志愿者招募`) into a flow/cut/MCMF network.
> - **Direction B (Flow $\to$ Non-Flow Optimization when $N \le 10^5$)**: Recognizing that a problem *is* a Max-Flow, Bipartite Matching, or MCMF problem at heart, and then using **Max-Flow Min-Cut Duality** (`CF 724E — Goods Transportation`: solving Max-Flow via $O(N^2)$ DP on the Min-Cut partition!), **Hall's Marriage Theorem** (`ARC 076 F`: reducing matching defect $\max_S (|S| - |N(S)|)$ to a Segment Tree!), **Planar Dual Shortest Paths**, or **Simulated Cost Flow (`模拟费用流` — Priority Queues / Slope Trick / WQS Binary Search)** to solve it in $O(N \log N)$ without ever running Dinic or SSP!
