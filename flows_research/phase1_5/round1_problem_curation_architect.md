### Chapter 01: Residual Networks, Augmenting Paths & The Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem

* **Core Structural Theme**: Skew-symmetric flow conservation, residual network $G_f = (V, E_f)$ with backward undo edges $c_f(v, u) = f(u, v)$, Ford–Fulkerson augmenting paths, the Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem ($\max |f| = \min_{(S, T)} c(S, T)$), flow integrality, and $s$-$t$ path-cycle flow decomposition.
* **Prerequisite DAG**: None (Entry Point).
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 1A` (`Iter 01`, `Iter 43`), `Wave 5B` (`Iter 100`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `1.1` Skew-Symmetric Flow Formulation & Cut Value Identity $|f| = f(S, T) = \sum_{u \in S, v \in T} f(u, v) - \sum_{u \in S, v \in T} f(v, u)$.
  * `1.2` Why Greedy Path Packing Fails (The Diamond Graph Counterexample) & The Residual Undo Edge $c_f(v, u) = f(u, v)$.
  * `1.3` Constructive Proof of the Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem via Residual Reachability $S = \{v \in V : s \rightsquigarrow_{G_f} v\}$.
  * `1.4` Integrality Theorem, Flow Decomposition into $\le |E|$ $s$-$t$ Paths and Directed Cycles, and 2-Commodity Symmetric Switching (`[CQOI2014] 危桥`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Indistinguishable conserved units moving through bottlenecked transitions ($N, M \le 500$) where committing an early path can block two intersecting optimal paths.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Flow vector $f \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^E$ satisfying $\sum_{v} f(u, v) - \sum_{w} f(w, u) = 0$ ($\forall u \neq s, t$) and $0 \le f(e) \le c(e)$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Map conservation nodes to vertices, capacities to directed edges, and time/exchange stages to layered residual arcs.
  4. **Failure Audit**: Omitting reverse residual edges $(v, u)$ traps greedy augmentation at $1$ on the $4$-vertex diamond network; naive 2-commodity flow allows commodity cross-over unless tested under both $(s_1, t_1; s_2, t_2)$ and $(s_1, t_1; t_2, s_2)$.
* **Flagship Worked Example**: **Luogu P3163 — [CQOI2014] 危桥** (`#007`, showing how $\frac{1}{2}(f_1 + f_2)$ and $\frac{1}{2}(f_1 - f_2)$ cancel cross-commodity interference on undirected networks).

### Chapter 02: Blocking Flows, Dinic's Algorithm & Unit-Network Complexity Proofs

* **Core Structural Theme**: BFS level graph $L_G$, DFS blocking flow with dead-end pointer pruning (`cur[u]` arc), strict monotonically increasing $d_{G_f}(s, t)$ per phase, $O(V^2 E)$ general bound, $O(E \min(V^{2/3}, E^{1/2}))$ unit-capacity bound, and $O(E\sqrt{V})$ unit-vertex/bipartite bound.
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 01`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 1A` (`Iter 02`, `Iter 43`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `2.1` Level Graph $L_G$, Blocking Flow Definition & Why Missing `cur[u]` Degrades DFS to $O(V E^2)$.
  * `2.2` Proof of Strict Distance Monotonicity $d_{G_{f'}}(s, t) \ge d_{G_f}(s, t) + 1$ Across Phases.
  * `2.3` Even–Tarjan Potential Proofs: $O(E \min(V^{2/3}, E^{1/2}))$ for Unit-Capacity Networks and $O(E\sqrt{V})$ for Unit-Vertex Networks.
  * `2.4` Worst-Case Anti-Dinic Pathological Graphs & Highest-Label Push-Relabel (`HLPP`) Comparison.
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Graph size $V, E \sim 10^4 - 10^5$ where $O(V^2 E)$ would TLE unequivocally, requiring the solver to prove unit-capacity or unit-vertex structure to unlock $O(E\sqrt{V})$.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Bound the phase threshold $K$: after $K$ blocking-flow phases, the residual distance satisfies $d(s, t) > K$, so the pigeonhole principle forces a thin layer $V_d$ separating $s$ and $t$ of residual cut capacity $\le |E| / K$ or $|V| / K$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Discretize geometric regions or tree paths so that internal edges have capacity $\infty$ and boundary/node capacities are $1$ (or small integers).
  4. **Failure Audit**: Resetting `cur[u]` inside the DFS loop instead of once per BFS phase destroys the $O(VE)$ blocking-flow guarantee.
* **Flagship Worked Example**: **Codeforces 786 E — ALT** (`#013`, demonstrating how HLD + segment tree virtual nodes yield $V = O(N \log N)$, $E = O(N \log^2 N)$ on a unit network where Even–Tarjan guarantees $< 0.2$s runtime).

### Chapter 04: Residual Cut Canonicalization, Unique Min-Cuts & Incremental Retraction

* **Core Structural Theme**: Residual SCC decomposition (`[AHOI2009] 最小割`), canonical $S$-minimal cut $S_{\min}$ and $T$-minimal cut $S_{\max}$, feasibility vs. mandatoriness of cut edges, lexicographically first minimum cuts via greedy flow retraction (`退流`), and $2^K$ Gray-code incremental residual search (`CF 1383 F`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 01`, `Ch 02`, `Ch 03`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 1A` (`Iter 04`, `Iter 43`, `Iter 45`), `Wave 2B` (`Iter 24`), `Wave 3A` (`Iter 24`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `4.1` Residual Equivalence Classes: Why Saturated Edges $(u, v)$ Belong to *Some* Min-Cut iff $\text{scc}_{G_f}(u) \neq \text{scc}_{G_f}(v)$ and *Every* Min-Cut iff $\text{scc}_{G_f}(u) = \text{scc}_{G_f}(s) \land \text{scc}_{G_f}(v) = \text{scc}_{G_f}(t)$.
  * `4.2` Testing Minimum-Cut Uniqueness in $O(E)$ Time via $V_S \cup V_T = V$ (`ZOJ 2587`).
  * `4.3` Surgical Flow Retraction (`退流` / `Un-Pushing`): Removing a Saturated Edge $(u, v)$ of Flow $f_0$ by Pushing $f_0$ Along $t \rightsquigarrow v$ and $u \rightsquigarrow s$ Without Re-running Dinic (`[SDOI2014] LIS`).
  * `4.4` Base-Network Freezing & $2^K$ Gray-Code Single-Edge Residual Transitions (`CF 1383 F — Special Edges`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: The problem asks *which* edges can/must appear in a minimum cut, whether the minimum cut is *unique*, which minimum cut is *lexicographically smallest*, or asks for max-flow across $Q \le 2\times 10^5$ capacity mutations on $K \le 10$ special edges.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: The family of all minimum $s$-$t$ cuts forms a distributive lattice under $(S_1 \cap S_2, S_1 \cup S_2)$, isomorphic to the order ideals of the quotient poset $G_f / \text{SCC}$ via Birkhoff's Representation Theorem.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Run Dinic once on the base network; run Tarjan's SCC on $G_f$ for cut classification, or retract flow along $t \to v$ and $u \to s$ to greedily lock lexicographically earliest cut edges.
  4. **Failure Audit**: Naive test of $\text{scc}(u) = \text{scc}(s)$ for "some min-cut" fails whenever an intermediate SCC exists between $\text{scc}(s)$ and $\text{scc}(t)$; only $f(u, v) = c(u, v) \land \text{scc}(u) \neq \text{scc}(v)$ is exact.

### Chapter 06: Hall's Marriage Theorem, Deficiency Form & Structural Submodular Applications

* **Core Structural Theme**: Hall's Marriage Condition ($\forall S \subseteq L, |N(S)| \ge |S|$), Ore's Deficiency Formula ($\nu(G) = |L| - \max_{S \subseteq L} (|S| - |N(S)|)$), submodularity of $|N(S)|$, interval Hall reduction to contiguous subsegments, SOS DP over bitmask neighborhoods, and constructive tree realization (`AGC 029 F`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 05`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 1B` (`Iter 08`, `Iter 44`), `Wave 3A` (`Iter 22`, `Iter 25`), `Wave 5B` (`Iter 98`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `6.1` Hall's Theorem & Ore's Deficiency Theorem Derived Directly from Max-Flow Min-Cut on $S \to L \to R \to T$.
  * `6.2` Convexity Reduction: Why Hall's Condition on Interval / Prefix-Suffix Bipartite Graphs Reduces from $2^N$ Subsets to $O(N^2)$ Contiguous Intervals (`[POI2009] LYZ`, `ARC 076 F`, `CF 981 F`).
  * `6.3` Exponential-to-Bitmask Compression: Checking $2^{|\Sigma|}$ Hall Subsets via SOS DP (`ARC 106 E`, `CF 1009 G`).
  * `6.4` Submodularity of Hall Tight Sets ($|N(A \cup B)| + |N(A \cap B)| \le |N(A)| + |N(B)|$) & Hyperedge-to-Tree Realization (`AGC 029 F`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: One side of the bipartite graph is either huge ($|R| \sim 10^9$, e.g. timeline days or skate sizes) with interval adjacency, or tiny ($|L| \le 18$ or $|\Sigma| = 6$) while the other side is $10^5$, making explicit Dinic impossible.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Replace $\nu(G)$ with $\min_{S \subseteq L} (|L \setminus S| + |N(S)|)$, and exploit structural closure (intervals, complements of intervals, or bitmasks) to optimize $|S| - |N(S)|$ algebraically.

### Chapter 07: Posets, Dilworth's Theorem, Chain Decomposition & Transitive Closure Nuances

* **Core Structural Theme**: Partially Ordered Sets (Posets), Dilworth's Theorem ($\text{width}(P) = \min \text{chain cover}$), Mirsky's Dual Theorem ($\text{height}(P) = \min \text{antichain partition}$), vertex-disjoint vs. general (overlapping) DAG path covers via transitive closure, weighted Dilworth antichains, and Aho–Corasick substring poset compression.
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 05`, `Ch 06`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 1B` (`Iter 09`, `Iter 10`, `Iter 44`), `Wave 5B` (`Iter 98`, `Iter 100`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `7.1` Vertex-Disjoint DAG Path Cover via Bipartite Split $G_{\text{split}} = (V_{\text{out}}, V_{\text{in}}, E)$: Why $\text{PathCover}_{\text{disjoint}}(D) = |V| - \nu(G_{\text{split}})$.
  * `7.2` General (Overlapping) Path Cover & Poset Transitive Closure: Why Triangle Shortcuts $1 \to 2 \to 3, 1 \to 3$ Break Disjoint Cover Unless Transitive Closure $D^+$ is Computed First.
  * `7.3` Constructive Maximum Antichain Extraction from König's Minimum Vertex Cover $C^*$ on $G_{\text{split}}$: $A^* = \{v \in V : v_{\text{out}} \notin C^* \land v_{\text{in}} \notin C^*\}$ (`[CTSC2008] 祭祀`).
  * `7.4` Weighted Dilworth Theorem (`ABC 354 G`) & Substring Containment Poset Compression via Aho–Corasick Fail Links (`CF 590 E`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Minimize chains/agents covering a DAG, OR maximize mutually incomparable elements (antichain: no $x \prec y$ pair selected).
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Every matched edge $(u_{\text{out}}, v_{\text{in}})$ in $G_{\text{split}}$ merges two chains into one, reducing the chain count from $|V|$ by $1$ per matched edge: $k = |V| - \nu(G_{\text{split}})$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Check if paths may share vertices: if **NO**, build $G_{\text{split}}$ directly on $E$; if **YES** (or if finding an antichain), compute transitive closure $E^+$ first, then match on $G_{\text{split}}(E^+)$.

### Chapter 09: Project Selection, Maximum Weight Closure & Pseudo-Boolean Cut Polynomials (`燃やす埋める`)

* **Core Structural Theme**: Picard's Maximum Weight Closure (`最大权闭合子图`), Japanese `燃やす埋める` (`Burn or Bury`) cut tables, pseudo-Boolean cut polynomials $f(\mathbf{x}) = C_0 + \sum_u a_u (1 - x_u) + \sum_v b_v x_v + \sum_{(u,v)} c_{uv} x_u (1 - x_v)$, collective subset bonus/penalty hubs, Kahn's topological cycle-deadlock pruning (`[NOI2009] 植物大战僵尸`), and Hall-pedestal closure (`CF 103 E`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 01`–`Ch 04`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 2A` (`Iter 16`, `Iter 17`, `Iter 45`), `Wave 5B` (`Iter 98`, `Iter 100`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `9.1` Algebraic Derivation of Project Selection: Rewriting $\max \sum_{v \in V} w_v x_v$ s.t. $x_u \le x_v$ as $\sum_{w_v > 0} w_v - \min_{\text{cut}} \left(\sum_{w_v > 0} w_v(1 - x_v) + \sum_{w_v < 0} |w_v| x_v + \sum_{u \to v} \infty \cdot x_u(1 - x_v)\right)$.
  * `9.2` Collective Subset Hubs: Compiling $\text{All-in-S Bonus } B \cdot \prod_{i \in A} x_i$ via $S \xrightarrow{B} H_A \xrightarrow{\infty} i$ and $\text{All-in-T Bonus } B \cdot \prod_{i \in A} (1 - x_i)$ via $i \xrightarrow{\infty} H'_A \xrightarrow{B} T$.
  * `9.3` Causal Deadlock Pruning: Why Positive-Weight Nodes Behind Directed Cycles Corrupt Closure Cuts Unless Pruned by Kahn's In-Degree-$0$ BFS (`[NOI2009] 植物大战僵尸`).
  * `9.4` Hall-Constrained Closure via $+M$ Pedestal Shift (`CF 103 E — Buying Sets`) & Divisor-DAG Sparsification (`CF 1473 F`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Binary accept/reject choices ($x_i \in \{0, 1\}$) with arbitrary positive profits and negative costs, governed by "if you take $A$, you must also take $B$" implications ($x_A \le x_B$).
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Separate positive rewards $W^+ = \sum_{w_i > 0} w_i$ (assumed collected upfront) from forfeited rewards $w_i(1 - x_i)$ and incurred costs $|w_j| x_j$, enforcing $x_u \le x_v \iff x_u(1 - x_v) = 0$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Set $x_i = 1 \iff i \in S$ and $x_i = 0 \iff i \in T$; wire $S \xrightarrow{w_i} i$ ($w_i > 0$), $j \xrightarrow{|w_j|} T$ ($w_j < 0$), and $u \xrightarrow{\infty} v$ ($x_u \le x_v$).
  4. **Failure Audit**: In `[NOI2009] 植物大战僵尸`, running only Tarjan SCC deletion leaves acyclic descendants of a 2-cycle unpruned; putting $i \in S$ for those nodes pays $0$ cut cost and falsely collects their positive score!

### Chapter 10: Submodularity, Bipartite Variable Inversion & Roof Duality (QPBO)

* **Core Structural Theme**: Submodular set functions $f(A) + f(B) \ge f(A \cup B) + f(A \cap B)$, 2-variable submodularity test $E(0,0) + E(1,1) \le E(0,1) + E(1,0)$ ($A + D \le B + C$), degree-identity node elimination (`[BZOJ 2039] 人员雇佣`), Bipartite Variable Inversion $y_v \gets 1 - x_v$ for rescuing supermodular $A + D > B + C$ costs on bipartite conflict graphs (`ABC 193 F`, `AGC 038 F`), and Hammer–Boros–Kolmogorov Roof Duality / QPBO (`CF 1666 K`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 09`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 2A` (`Iter 18`, `Iter 19`, `Iter 20`, `Iter 45`), `Wave 2B` (`Iter 46`), `Wave 4B` (`Iter 49`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `10.1` The Exact Submodularity Condition: Why a Pairwise Penalty Table $\begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{pmatrix}$ Compiles to Non-Negative Cut Capacities iff $A + D \le B + C$.
  * `10.2` Degree-Identity Node Elimination: Replacing $O(N^2)$ Auxiliary Edge Vertices with Half-Sum Source/Sink Capacities (`[BZOJ 2039] 人员雇佣`).
  * `10.3` Rescuing Supermodularity ($A + D > B + C$) via Bipartite Variable Inversion $y_v = 1 - x_v$ on 2-Colorable Graphs (`Zebraness`, `圈地计划`, `AGC 038 F`).
  * `10.4` Non-Bipartite Supermodular Rescue & 3-State $\{A, B, \emptyset\}$ Partition via Doubled-Variable QPBO / Roof Duality (`CF 1666 K`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Binary choices with pairwise interaction tables ("if $u, v$ choose the same state vs. different states"), especially when the problem *rewards* disagreement or *penalizes* agreement ($A + D > B + C$).
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Write the pairwise energy table $\begin{pmatrix} E_{00} & E_{01} \\ E_{10} & E_{11} \end{pmatrix}$; compute $\Delta = E_{01} + E_{10} - E_{00} - E_{11}$. If $\Delta \ge 0$, decompose directly into unary + $u \leftrightarrow v$ cut terms; if $\Delta < 0$, check if the interaction graph is bipartite and apply $y_v = 1 - x_v$ on one shore to flip $\Delta \gets -\Delta > 0$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Under $y_v = 1 - x_v$ for $v \in R$, state $x_v = 0$ maps to $v \in S$ ($y_v = 1$) and $x_v = 1$ maps to $v \in T$ ($y_v = 0$), reversing source/sink meanings on $R$ and converting same-state conflicts into directed $(u \in S, v \in T)$ cut edges.
  4. **Failure Audit**: Applying bipartite variable flip to an odd cycle (e.g. $K_3$) leaves at least one monochromatic edge with negative capacity $-\Delta < 0$; on non-bipartite graphs with $3$ states $\{A, B, \emptyset\}$, only QPBO doubled variables $(u_1, u_2)$ work.
* **Flagship Worked Example**: **AtCoder AGC 038 F — Two Permutations** (`#095`, decomposing $P$ and $Q$ into disjoint cycles, formulating the $2\times 2$ co-rotation reward table per index $i$, and flipping $Q$-cycle indicators $y_d \gets 1 - y_d$ to turn supermodular co-fixed rewards into a submodular min-cut).

### Chapter 11: Multi-State Unary Chains, Monge Matrices & Constrained Cut Gadgets (`切糕` Archetype)

* **Core Structural Theme**: Multi-state discrete variables $x_i \in \{1, \dots, K\}$, unary threshold encoding $z_{i,t} = [x_i \ge t]$ with monotonicity chain $1 = z_{i,1} \ge z_{i,2} \ge \dots \ge z_{i,K} \ge z_{i,K+1} = 0$, mandatory reverse $\infty$-edges $(i, t+1) \xrightarrow{\infty} (i, t)$, Lipschitz / distance-$D$ cross-edges (`[HNOI2013] 切糕`), 3-state component cuts (`ARC 107 F`), hidden Deficit/Surplus bipartitions (`ARC 142 E`), and order-reversed bipartite chains (`yukicoder 1541`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 09`, `Ch 10`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 2A` (`Iter 21`, `Iter 45`), `Wave 2B` (`Iter 21`, `Iter 22`, `Iter 46`), `Wave 4B` (`Iter 49`), `Wave 5B` (`Iter 100`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `11.1` Unary Threshold Chains & Why Omitting Reverse $\infty$-Edges $(i, t+1) \xrightarrow{\infty} (i, t)$ Allows Multi-Cut Cheat States.
  * `11.2` Cross-Chain Difference Constraints $x_u - x_v \le D$ Compiled as $(u, t) \xrightarrow{\infty} (v, t - D)$ (`[HNOI2013] 切糕` & `FoxAndCity`).
  * `11.3` 3-State Sign/Deletion Cuts $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ via 2-Edge Vertex Chains (`ARC 107 F`) and 4-Stage Grid Parity Chains (`CF 1517 G`).
  * `11.4` Hidden Bipartite Role Splitting (`ARC 142 E — Pairing Wizards`) & Order-Reversed Bipartite Supermodular Chains (`yukicoder 1541`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Each variable $x_i$ chooses from $K \ge 3$ ordered levels ($K \le 100$) with arbitrary unary level costs $f_i(x_i)$ and pairwise difference bounds $x_u - x_v \le D$ or convex interaction costs $\Phi(x_u - x_v)$.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Replace $x_i \in \{1, \dots, K\}$ with $K-1$ binary threshold indicators $z_{i,t} = [x_i > t] \in \{0, 1\}$ subject to $z_{i,1} \ge z_{i,2} \ge \dots \ge z_{i,K-1}$; then $x_u - x_v \le D \iff \forall t, z_{u,t} \le z_{v,t-D}$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Build a directed chain $S = (i, 0) \xrightarrow{f_i(1)} (i, 1) \xrightarrow{f_i(2)} \dots \xrightarrow{f_i(K)} (i, K) = T$ with reverse edges $(i, t) \xrightarrow{\infty} (i, t-1)$ and cross-edges $(u, t) \xrightarrow{\infty} (v, t-D)$.
  4. **Failure Audit**: Without $(i, t) \xrightarrow{\infty} (i, t-1)$, a cross-edge entering $(i, t)$ and exiting $(i, t-2)$ can be severed by cutting *two* cheap edges on chain $i$—corrupting the $1$-to-$1$ bijection between cuts and variable assignments!

### Chapter 12: The Discrete Coarea Formula, Threshold Layer Decomposition & $L_1$ Smoothing

* **Core Structural Theme**: The Discrete Coarea Identity $|x_u - x_v| = \sum_{t=1}^{K-1} |[x_u > t] - [x_v > t]|$, convex finite-difference kernel $\Phi(x_u - x_v) = \sum_{s, t} \Delta^2 \Phi(s, t) [x_u > s][x_v \le t]$ (Ishikawa's Construction), continuous Coarea collapse to binary cuts (`[NOI2010] 海拔`), and incremental residual rollback across Coarea level sets (`CF 1427 G — One Billion Shades of Grey`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 11`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 2B` (`Iter 22`, `Iter 23`, `Iter 24`, `Iter 46`), `Wave 3A` (`Iter 23`), `Wave 4B` (`Iter 49`), `Wave 5B` (`Iter 100`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `12.1` The Discrete Coarea Formula: Layer Cake Decomposition $\sum_{(u,v) \in E} w_{uv} |x_u - x_v| = \sum_{t=1}^{K-1} (\lambda_{t+1} - \lambda_t) \cdot \text{Cut}_w(S_t)$.
  * `12.2` Ishikawa's Higher-Order Convex Edge Decomposition: Compiling $(x_u - x_v)^2$ and General Convex $\Phi(x_u - x_v)$ via Second-Order Differences $\Delta^2 \Phi$ (`ABC 347 G`, `ARC 129 E`).
  * `12.3` Pedestal-Shifted Non-Monotone Unary Potentials $M - f_i(t)$ on Multi-State Chains (`CF 434 D`).
  * `12.4` Decoupled Coarea Sweeping + Surgical Flow Rollback in $O(N^3)$ (`CF 1427 G — One Billion Shades of Grey`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Minimize $\sum_{(u,v) \in E} w_{uv} |x_u - x_v|$ or $\sum_{(u,v) \in E} \Phi(x_u - x_v)$ over integer or continuous values $x_u \in [1, 10^9]$ with fixed boundary pins.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Apply $|x_u - x_v| = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} |[x_u > \lambda] - [x_v > \lambda]| \, d\lambda$; when there are no unary terms (or unary terms are convex/separable), each threshold $\lambda_k$ decouples into an independent binary min-cut!
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: If $K \le 5$ with unary terms (`ABC 347 G`), build the coupled $K$-layer Ishikawa graph; if $V_{\max} = 10^9$ with pure $L_1$ smoothing (`CF 1427 G`), sweep the sorted boundary values $v_1 < v_2 < \dots < v_K$ on a *single* binary graph, rolling back flow on $v_k \to T$ and pushing $S \to v_k$.
  4. **Failure Audit**: Building $K = 4N$ separate Dinic instances for `CF 1427 G` takes $O(N^4)$ and TLEs; only incremental residual rollback ($O(N)$ per boundary flip because boundary degree $\le 4$) achieves $O(N^3)$.
* **Flagship Worked Example**: **Codeforces 1427 G — One Billion Shades of Grey** (`#111`, proving the Discrete Coarea decoupling theorem and executing the 3-step surgical residual rollback as boundary pins cross $\lambda_k$).

### Chapter 13: Isotonic Regression ($L_1 / L_2 / L_p$), Matroid Exchange & Parametric Divide-and-Conquer (`整体二分`)

* **Core Structural Theme**: Isotonic Regression (`保序回归`, 2018 National Team Paper by 高睿泉): minimizing $\sum_{v \in V} w_v |x_v - y_v|^p$ subject to DAG partial-order constraints $x_u \le x_v$ ($\forall (u, v) \in E_{\text{DAG}}$), convex subgradient threshold cuts $c_v(\lambda) = w_v ((\lambda + \epsilon - y_v)^p - (\lambda - y_v)^p)$, nested cut monotonicity $S^*(\lambda_1) \subseteq S^*(\lambda_2)$, Parallel Binary Search (`整体二分`) in $O(T_{\text{flow}}(V, E) \log U)$, Inverse MST in $L_1/L_2$ norms, and Matroid Basis Exchange DAGs (`[省选联考 2020] 魔法商店`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 09`, `Ch 11`, `Ch 12`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 2B` (`Iter 46`), `Wave 3A` (`Iter 21`, `Iter 47`), `Wave 4B` (`Iter 49`, `Iter 90`), `Wave 5B` (`Iter 96`, `Iter 98`, `Iter 100`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `13.1` Continuous & Discrete Coarea Derivation of $L_p$ Isotonic Regression: Why Threshold Cut $z_v(\lambda) = [x_v > \lambda]$ Satisfies Strict Nested Monotonicity $S^*(\lambda_1) \subseteq S^*(\lambda_2)$.
  * `13.2` Parallel Binary Search (`整体二分`) on the Quotient DAG: Splitting Active Vertex Set $V_{\text{cur}}$ into $V_L = V_{\text{cur}} \cap T$ ($x_v^* \le \text{mid}$) and $V_R = V_{\text{cur}} \cap S$ ($x_v^* \ge \text{mid}+1$) so Total Flow Size per Depth is $\sum |V_k| = |V|$.
  * `13.3` Inverse Minimum Spanning Tree Dichotomy: $L_1$ Tree/Non-Tree Exchange (`[SHOI2004]`) vs. $L_2$ Isotonic Regression (`[BJWC2018]`).
  * `13.4` Matroid Basis Exchange Graph Construction (`Bruhat–Hall` Bijection) + Isotonic Regression (`[省选联考 2020 A 卷] 魔法商店`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Adjust vertex values $x_i$ from initial targets $y_i$ at minimum $\sum w_i |x_i - y_i|^p$ cost so that a partial order $x_u \le x_v$ holds (explicitly given, or induced by forcing a target spanning tree / linear basis $A$ to be minimum-weight and $B$ to be maximum-weight).
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: For any test midpoint $\lambda$, compute marginal directional cost $\Delta_i(\lambda) = f_i(\lambda + 1) - f_i(\lambda)$ (or derivative $f'_i(\lambda)$ for continuous $x_i$); minimize $\sum_{i \in V_{\text{cur}}} \Delta_i(\lambda) z_i$ subject to $z_u \le z_v$ ($\forall (u, v) \in E_{\text{DAG}}[V_{\text{cur}}]$).
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: If $\Delta_i(\lambda) < 0$ (prefer $x_i \ge \lambda+1$), wire $S \xrightarrow{-\Delta_i(\lambda)} i$; if $\Delta_i(\lambda) > 0$ (prefer $x_i \le \lambda$), wire $i \xrightarrow{\Delta_i(\lambda)} T$; wire $u \xrightarrow{\infty} v$ for $x_u \le x_v$ ($z_u \le z_v$). Recurse on $V \cap T$ for $[L, \lambda]$ and $V \cap S$ for $[\lambda+1, R]$.
  4. **Failure Audit**: Keeping edges between $V_L$ and $V_R$ across recursive calls of `整体二分` makes each recursion depth take $O(|V|)$ vertices per call ($O(|V| \log U)$ calls $\to$ TLE); dropping cross-partition edges is valid precisely because $x_u \le \lambda < \lambda + 1 \le x_v$ is already permanently satisfied!
* **Flagship Worked Example**: **Luogu P6621 / LOJ 3303 — [省选联考 2020 A 卷] 魔法商店 & 保序回归** (`#117` & `#118`, building the XOR linear-basis exchange DAG from $A$ and $B$, then executing `整体二分` min-cuts).

### Chapter 14: Fractional Programming, Dinkelbach's Method & Maximum Density Subgraphs

* **Core Structural Theme**: 0-1 Fractional Programming $\max_{\mathbf{x} \in \mathcal{F}} \frac{A(\mathbf{x})}{B(\mathbf{x})}$ ($B(\mathbf{x}) > 0$), parametric linearization $h(\lambda) = \max_{\mathbf{x} \in \mathcal{F}} (A(\mathbf{x}) - \lambda B(\mathbf{x}))$, convexity and strict monotonicity of $h(\lambda)$, Dinkelbach's Newton-secant iteration vs. binary search, Goldberg's Degree-Identity Maximum Density Subgraph (`Hard Life`), Amber (胡伯涛 2007) National Team Paper taxonomy, and Nash-Williams Arboricity / Forest Packing.
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 09`, `Ch 10`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 3A` (`Iter 21`, `Iter 47`), `Wave 1A` (`Iter 05`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `14.1` Parametric Equivalence Theorem: $A(\mathbf{x})/B(\mathbf{x}) \ge \lambda \iff h(\lambda) = \max_{\mathbf{x}} (A(\mathbf{x}) - \lambda B(\mathbf{x})) \ge 0$, and Superlinear Convergence of Dinkelbach's Secant Update $\lambda_{k+1} = A(\mathbf{x}^*_k) / B(\mathbf{x}^*_k)$.
  * `14.2` Minimum Ratio $s$-$t$ Cut (`[TJOI2012] 网络严密性`): Why Negative Parametric Edge Weights $w_e(\lambda) = c_e - \lambda t_e < 0$ Are Absorbed Upfront Before Running Dinic on $\{e : w_e(\lambda) > 0\}$.
  * `14.3` Goldberg's Degree-Identity Transformation for Maximum Density Subgraphs (`Hard Life`): Rewriting $2|E(S)| = \sum_{u \in S} d_u - |E(S, \bar{S})|$ to Eliminate $O(M)$ Edge Nodes.
  * `14.4` Nash-Williams Forest Packing Theorem ($\max_{\emptyset \neq S, |S| \ge 2} \frac{|E(S)|}{|S| - 1} \le k$) & Rooted Density Cuts.
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Maximize or minimize a *ratio* (average density $|E(S)|/|S|$, profit-to-cost $\sum p_i / \sum c_i$, or maximum out-degree $\lceil |E(S)|/|S| \rceil$) over a combinatorial family (cuts, closures, matchings, subgraphs).
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Introduce guess $\lambda \ge 0$ and test $\max_{\mathbf{x}} (A(\mathbf{x}) - \lambda B(\mathbf{x})) \ge 0$. For subgraph density, use $2 \sum_{e \in E(S)} w_e - 2\lambda \sum_{v \in S} c_v = \sum_{v \in S} (d_v - 2\lambda c_v) - w(S, \bar{S})$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Fix pedestal $U = \max_v d_v$; wire $S \xrightarrow{U} v \xrightarrow{U + 2\lambda c_v - d_v} T$ and undirected $(u, v)$ of capacity $w_{uv}$; then $S \neq \emptyset$ is recovered from the residual reachability set $V_S \setminus \{S\}$.
  4. **Failure Audit**: In Maximum Density Subgraph (`Hard Life`), the empty set $S = \emptyset$ always achieves $A(\emptyset) - \lambda B(\emptyset) = 0$! Because $|S|$ is an integer $\in [1, N]$, any two distinct densities differ by at least $\frac{1}{N(N-1)}$, so binary search with $\epsilon = \frac{1}{2 N^2}$ and checking $V_S \setminus \{S\} \neq \emptyset$ avoids the $\emptyset$ trap.
* **Flagship Worked Example**: **UVA 1389 / POJ 3155 — Hard Life** (`#129`, deriving Goldberg's degree-identity network from Amber's 2007 monograph and proving the $\frac{1}{N(N-1)}$ precision spacing bound).

### Chapter 15: Planar Graph Duality, Cycle–Cut Orthogonality & Multi-Terminal Planar Cuts

* **Core Structural Theme**: Planar embeddings and Euler's formula ($V - E + F = 2$), Cycle–Cut Orthogonality ($\text{Cut}(G) \cong \text{Cycle}(G^*)$), $s$-$t$ Planar Minimum Cut via dual shortest path (`[BJWC2006] 狼抓兔子`), Directed Planar Duality via $90^\circ$ clockwise rotation (`[NOI2010] 海拔`), Reif's divide-and-conquer interior $s$-$t$ planar cut, and multi-terminal outerplanar boundary cuts via dual Dijkstra + circular interval DP (`[CSP-S 2021] 交通规划`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 01`, `Ch 12`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 3A` (`Iter 22`, `Iter 23`, `Iter 47`), `Wave 4B` (`Iter 49`), `Wave 5B` (`Iter 100`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `15.1` Outerplanar $s$-$t$ Minimum Cut (`s, t` on Outer Face): Adding Artificial Infinity Arc $t \to s$ Splits $f_{\infty}$ into $F_{\text{left}}$ and $F_{\text{right}}$, Bijecting Minimal $s$-$t$ Cuts to Simple Paths $F_{\text{left}} \rightsquigarrow F_{\text{right}}$ in $G^*$.
  * `15.2` Directed Planar Duality (`[NOI2010] 海拔`): Why Rotating Primal Directed Edge $u \to v$ by $90^\circ$ Clockwise Maps Forward Cut Edges $(u \in S, v \in T)$ to Directed Dual Edges $F_{\text{left}} \to F_{\text{right}}$.
  * `15.3` Interior $s$-$t$ Planar Minimum Cut ($s, t$ Not on Same Face): Reif's Shortest-Path Slicing & Monge Divide-and-Conquer in $O(N \log^2 N)$.
  * `15.4` Multi-Terminal Outerplanar Cuts (`[CSP-S 2021] 交通规划`): Boundary Color Transitions, $2c$ Dual Dijkstras, and Non-Crossing Circular Interval Matching DP.
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Minimum cut (or continuous/multi-state $L_1$ altitude smoothing) on a planar graph or 2D grid where $V, E \sim 2.5 \times 10^5 - 2 \times 10^6$, far beyond Dinic's time limit, with terminals on the outer boundary.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: By Jordan's Curve Theorem and Cycle–Cut Duality, any minimal cut separating boundary intervals is a collection of non-crossing dual paths connecting the boundary transition faces $F_1, \dots, F_{2c}$ in $G^*$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Triangulate/index the faces of $G$; orient each dual edge $e^* = (f_L \to f_R)$ by rotating $e = (u \to v)$ $90^\circ$ clockwise with weight $c(u, v)$, and run Dijkstra in $O(F \log F)$.
  4. **Failure Audit**: Reversing the $90^\circ$ rotation (counterclockwise instead of clockwise) swaps $s$-$t$ cuts with $t$-$s$ cuts on directed planar graphs (`[NOI2010] 海拔`); forgetting the $N=1$ or $M=1$ degenerate grid check in `[BJWC2006] 狼抓兔子` outputs $0$ instead of $\min_e w_e$ because there are $0$ interior faces!
* **Flagship Worked Example**: **Luogu P7916 — [CSP-S 2021] 交通规划** (`#143`, showing how $k \le 50$ alternating-color boundary terminals induce $2c$ boundary transition faces in $G^*$ whose pairwise dual shortest paths are paired without crossing via $O(c^3)$ circular interval DP).

### Chapter 16: Global Minimum Cuts, Gomory–Hu Cut Trees & Min-Cut Cactus Representations

* **Core Structural Theme**: Global Undirected Minimum Cut $\lambda(G) = \min_{\emptyset \neq S \subsetneq V} c(S, \bar{S})$, Stoer–Wagner Maximum Adjacency Search (`MAS`) $s$-$t$-of-the-phase theorem, Gomory–Hu / Gusfield All-Pairs Minimum Cut Trees ($N-1$ Dinic calls), Ultrametric Min-Cut Inequality $\lambda(u, w) \ge \min(\lambda(u, v), \lambda(v, w))$, and the Dinits–Karzanov–Lomonosov Cactus of All Global Minimum Cuts.
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 01`–`Ch 04`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 3A` (`Iter 25`, `Iter 47`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `16.1` Stoer–Wagner Global Minimum Cut: Proof that the Last Two Vertices $(s, t)$ of Maximum Adjacency Ordering (`MAS`) Satisfy $\lambda(s, t; G) = c(\{t\}, V \setminus \{t\})$.
  * `16.2` Submodularity & Uncrossing of Undirected Minimum Cuts: Proof that $s$-$t$ Min-Cuts Never Need to Cross, Guaranteeing the Existence of a Gomory–Hu Cut Tree $T_{\text{GH}}$.
  * `16.3` Gusfield's Simplified $N-1$ Max-Flow Algorithm (No Graph Contraction Required) & Distinct Min-Cut Value Bound ($\le N-1$).
  * `16.4` Crossing Global Min-Cuts & The Dinits–Karzanov–Lomonosov Cactus Representation ($O(N)$ Size Encoding Up to $\binom{N}{2}$ Cuts).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Undirected graph queries asking for global disconnectivity, all $\binom{N}{2}$ pairwise min-cuts $\lambda(u, v)$, the number of distinct pairwise min-cuts, or a permutation $\pi$ maximizing $\sum_{i=1}^{N-1} \lambda(\pi_i, \pi_{i+1})$.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Any function $\lambda: V \times V \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ arising as pairwise minimum cuts of an undirected graph satisfies the strong triangle (ultrametric) inequality $\lambda(u, w) \ge \min(\lambda(u, v), \lambda(v, w))$, which is realized by bottleneck paths on a unique weighted tree $T_{\text{GH}}$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Run Gusfield's loop for $i = 2 \dots N$: compute $\text{MinCut}(i, p[i])$, set tree edge $(i, p[i])$ weight to the cut value, and for every $j \in \{i+1 \dots N\}$ with $p[j] = p[i]$ and $j \in V_S$, update $p[j] \gets i$.
  4. **Failure Audit**: Updating $p[j] \gets i$ only for $j > i$ builds the correct cut-equivalent tree (preserving all-pairs $\lambda(u, v)$), whereas also updating components of $p[i]$ on the $S$-side is required if the tree edges themselves must correspond to actual partitions of $V$ (`CF 343 E`).
* **Flagship Worked Example**: **Codeforces 343 E — Pumping Stations** (`#149`, building the Gomory–Hu tree $T_{\text{GH}}$, proving $\sum_{i=1}^{N-1} \lambda(\pi_i, \pi_{i+1}) \le \sum_{e \in T_{\text{GH}}} w_e$, and achieving equality by recursively splitting on the minimum-weight tree edge).

### Chapter 17: Feasible Circulations with Lower Bounds ($[l, u]$), $s$-$t$ Min/Max Flow & Eulerian Orientations

* **Core Structural Theme**: Demand-bounded edges $l(e) \le f(e) \le u(e)$, baseline shift $f(e) = l(e) + f'(e)$ ($0 \le f'(e) \le u(e) - l(e)$), net node excess $M(v) = \sum_{e \in \delta^-(v)} l(e) - \sum_{e \in \delta^+(v)} l(e)$, auxiliary super-terminals $(S^*, T^*)$, the 5 canonical lower-bound variants (無源匯可行流, 有源匯可行流, 有源匯最大流, 有源匯最小流, 有源匯最小費用可行流), Hoffman's Circulation Theorem, and Mixed-Graph Eulerian Orientation (`Sightseeing Tour`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 01`–`Ch 04`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 1A` (`Iter 05`, `Iter 43`), `Wave 3B` (`Iter 26`), `Wave 4A` (`Iter 31`), `Wave 4B` (`Iter 49`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `17.1` Algebraic Derivation of $M(v)$ from Conservation $\sum_{\text{in}}(l_e + f'_e) = \sum_{\text{out}}(l_e + f'_e)$ & Why $M(v) > 0$ Wires $S^* \xrightarrow{M(v)} v$ While $M(v) < 0$ Wires $v \xrightarrow{-M(v)} T^*$.
  * `17.2` Unified Master Recipe for All 5 Lower-Bound Variants via Feedback Loop $t \xrightarrow{[0, \infty)} s$:
    * Feasibility $\iff \text{dinic}(S^*, T^*) == \sum_{M(v) > 0} M(v)$;
    * Max-Flow $= \text{dinic}(s, t)$ on $G_f$ (after deleting $t \to s$ or keeping $f_0$);
    * Min-Flow $= f_0 - \text{dinic}(t, s)$ on $G_f$ (after deleting $t \to s$ and its reverse arc).
  * `17.3` Hoffman's Circulation Theorem ($\forall S \subseteq V, l(\delta^-(S)) \le u(\delta^+(S))$) & Mixed-Graph Eulerian Circuits (`POJ 1637`).
  * `17.4` Minimum-Cost Lower-Bound Circulations (`[AHOI2014] 支线剧情` & `CF 1288 F`): Pre-Paying $\sum l_e c_e$ and Running MCMF from $S^*$ to $T^*$.
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Every edge, vertex, or time window has *both* a mandatory minimum quota $l_e$ ("at least $k$ visits", "discrepancy $\le d$") and an upper limit $u_e$.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Substitute $f_e = l_e + f'_e$; rewrite conservation as $\text{out}(f', v) - \text{in}(f', v) = \text{in}(l, v) - \text{out}(l, v) = M(v)$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Add edge $(u, v)$ with capacity $u_e - l_e$; accumulate `M[v] += l_e` and `M[u] -= l_e`; after processing all edges (including $t \xrightarrow{[0, \infty)} s$ for $s$-$t$ networks), connect $S^* \to v$ for $M[v] > 0$ and $v \to T^*$ for $M[v] < 0$.
  4. **Failure Audit**: In Variant 4 ($s$-$t$ Minimum Flow), forgetting to zero out *both* the forward edge $(t, s)$ and the reverse edge $(s, t)$ before running $\text{dinic}(t, s)$ allows $\text{dinic}(t, s)$ to push flow right back through the artificial loop, outputting $0$!

### Chapter 18: Min-Cost Max-Flow (MCMF), Johnson Potentials, Convex Cost Splitting & Negative Cycles

* **Core Structural Theme**: Skew-symmetric edge costs $c(v, u) = -c(u, v)$, Negative-Cycle Optimality Theorem ($f$ is min-cost $\iff G_f$ has no negative-cost directed cycle), Successive Shortest Path (`SSP`) via SPFA vs. Primal–Dual Dijkstra with Johnson potentials $\pi(v)$ ($c_{\pi}(u, v) = c(u, v) + \pi(u) - \pi(v) \ge 0$), topological DAG potential initialization (`ABC 214 H`), separable convex cost marginal splitting ($\Delta C(k) = C(k) - C(k-1)$), and pre-injected negative-edge / circulation repair (`CF 708 D`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 01`–`Ch 03`, `Ch 17`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 3B` (`Iter 26`, `Iter 27`, `Iter 30`, `Iter 48`), `Wave 4A` (`Iter 32`), `Wave 4B` (`Iter 49`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `18.1` Skew-Symmetric Costs $c(v, u) = -c(u, v)$ & Proof of the Negative-Cycle Optimality Theorem via Circulation Decomposition.
  * `18.2` Johnson Potentials $\pi(v)$, Complementary Slackness $c_{\pi}(u, v) \ge 0$, and Topological Potential Initialization on Condensed SCC DAGs (`ABC 214 H`).
  * `18.3` Separable Convex Edge Costs: Marginal Difference Splitting $\Delta C(k) = C(k) - C(k-1)$ (`CF 863 F`, `[WC2007] 剪刀石头布`, `[JSOI2009] 球队收益`).
  * `18.4` Mandatory Negative-Edge Pre-Saturation & 3-Piece Convex Circulation Repair (`CF 708 D — Incorrect Flow`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Flow conservation with additive linear costs $\sum c_e f_e$ OR separable convex/quadratic penalties $\sum \Phi_e(f_e)$ (e.g. $\binom{w_i}{2}$, $c x_i^2$, waiting-time penalties) where greedy shortest paths get trapped without $-c_e$ undo arcs.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Replace any convex function $\Phi(x)$ ($x \in \{0, \dots, U\}$) with $U$ parallel unit-capacity edges of costs $\Delta_k = \Phi(k) - \Phi(k-1)$ satisfying $\Delta_1 \le \Delta_2 \le \dots \le \Delta_U$.

  4. **Failure Audit**: Applying marginal splitting to a *concave* cost ($\Delta_1 > \Delta_2$) causes MCMF to pick the $k=2$ edge *before* the $k=1$ edge, producing nonsense; in `ABC 214 H` ($N = 2\times 10^5$), running initial SPFA instead of topological DP on the condensed SCC DAG TLEs on grid-like DAGs.

### Chapter 19: Consecutive Equation Differencing ($DA\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$), Interval Packing & Ribbon Gadgets

* **Core Structural Theme**: Totally Unimodular (TU) Column-Consecutive-Ones linear programs, the Consecutive Equation-Differencing Transform $D(A\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{b})$ where $D_{i,i} = 1, D_{i,i-1} = -1$, exact column-sum zero conservation ($\sum_i (DA)_{i,j} = 0$ with a single $+1$ at $L_j$ and $-1$ at $R_j+1$), Weighted $k$-Interval Packing (`POJ 3680`), Flagship Volunteer Recruitment (`[NOI2008] 志愿者招募`), Two-Sided Window Bounds (`NEERC 2016 D`), and Tree Equation Differencing (`E_u - \sum_{v \in \text{ch}(u)} E_v`).

  * `19.1` Why Naive Interval Gadgets Fail & How Differencing $E_i - E_{i-1}$ Turns Column-Consecutive-Ones Matrices into Exact Flow Conservation Networks.
  * `19.2` Maximum-Weight $k$-Interval Packing (`Luogu P3358` / `POJ 3680`): Spine $i \xrightarrow{(k, 0)} i+1$ vs. Bypass Arc $L_j \xrightarrow{(1, -w_j)} R_j$.
  * `19.3` Minimum-Cost Interval Covering (`[NOI2008] 志愿者招募`): Deriving Forward Jump $L_j \xrightarrow{(\infty, c_j)} R_j + 1$ and Backward Slack $i+1 \xrightarrow{(\infty, 0)} i$ Purely from $E_i - E_{i-1} = 0$.
  * `19.4` Two-Sided Window Bounds (`NEERC 2016 D — Delight for a Cat`), Penalized Slacks (`ARC 137 E`), and Rooted Tree Equation Differencing (`LOJ #6405`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: Decision variables $x_j \ge 0$ each cover a contiguous interval $[L_j, R_j]$ (or a vertical tree path $v \to u$), and every time step $i \in [1, N]$ satisfies an inequality or two-sided bound on $\sum_{j \ni i} x_j$.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Convert every inequality at time $i$ into an **exact equality** by introducing bounded slack/surplus variable $y_i \in [0, U_i]$:
     $$E_i: \quad \sum_{j: L_j \le i \le R_j} x_j - y_i = a_i \quad (i = 1, \dots, N), \qquad E_0 \equiv 0, \quad E_{N+1} \equiv 0.$$
     Take consecutive differences $\Delta E_i = E_i - E_{i-1}$ for $i = 1, \dots, N+1$:
     $$\sum_{j: L_j = i} x_j - \sum_{j: R_j + 1 = i} x_j - y_i + y_{i-1} = a_i - a_{i-1}.$$
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Identify $+v$ as outgoing flow from vertex $i$ and $-v$ as incoming flow into vertex $i$:
     * Variable $x_j$ has $+x_j$ at $L_j$ and $-x_j$ at $R_j+1 \implies$ directed edge $L_j \xrightarrow{(u_j, c_j)} R_j + 1$;
     * Variable $y_i$ has $-y_i$ at $i$ and $+y_i$ at $i+1 \implies$ directed edge $i+1 \xrightarrow{(U_i, d_i)} i$;
     * RHS $b_i = a_i - a_{i-1}$ is node supply ($S^* \xrightarrow{b_i} i$ if $b_i > 0$, $i \xrightarrow{-b_i} T^*$ if $b_i < 0$).
  4. **Failure Audit**: Guessing the direction of the slack edge ($i \to i+1$ vs. $i+1 \to i$) without writing the sign of $-y_i + y_{i-1}$ reverses $\ge a_i$ into $\le a_i$!

  * `21.4` Edmonds' Matroid Intersection Theorem ($\max_{I \in \mathcal{I}_1 \cap \mathcal{I}_2} |I| = \min_{U \subseteq E} (r_1(U) + r_2(E \setminus U))$): Laminar Out-Tree/In-Tree Flow vs. Cunningham's Exchange-Graph Augmenting Paths (`CF 1284 G`, `CF 1556 H`).

  1. **Diagnostic Signal**:

     * Queries asking for the shortest path after distributing an arbitrary continuous budget $X$ to lengthen edges (`CF 1307 G`);
     * Spanning tree / forest constraints combined with color/vertex degree caps ($N \le 50$, `CF 1284 G`, `CF 1556 H`).
  2. **Algebraic Representation**:
     * If $A$ is row-consecutive-ones ($M \times N$), transpose via LP Duality so $A^T$ ($N \times M$) becomes *column*-consecutive-ones on $N$ rows!
     * For `CF 1307 G`, the dual of $\max \text{dist}(1, N)$ s.t. $\sum \Delta_e \le X$ is $\min_{f > 0} \frac{\text{Cost}(f) + X}{|f|}$ over $1$-$N$ flows $f$.

     * Difference $D A^T \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{0}$ into an $(N+1)$-vertex MCMF network (`[ZJOI2013] 防守战线`);

  4. **Failure Audit**: Differencing a *row*-consecutive-ones matrix directly creates $M = 10^4$ vertices instead of $N = 1000$ vertices; in matroid intersection, forgetting to take the *shortest* (minimum-hop / Bellman–Ford lexicographic) augmenting path creates chord shortcuts that violate independence!

* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: The underlying flow or MCMF formulation is conceptually clean, but the transition set $E = \{(u, v) : \text{pred}(u, v)\}$ has quadratic size $O(N^2)$ ($N \in [3000, 10^5]$) governed by intervals, 2D dominance, $\ell_1$ distances, or XOR bounds.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Factor the transition predicate or cost into separable endpoints:
     * Interval $[L_u, R_u] \implies \bigsqcup_{k=1}^{O(\log N)} I_k$ on a segment tree;

     * $\ell_1$ distance $\sum_{d=1}^D |x_{u,d} - x_{v,d}| = \max_{\mathbf{s} \in \{\pm 1\}^D} \left(\sum_d s_d x_{u,d} + \sum_d (-s_d) x_{v,d}\right)$ via $2^D$ sign hubs.

### Chapter 23: Eliminating Flow via DP, Convexity & WQS Binary Search (Min-Cut DP & Parametric Slopes)

* **Core Structural Theme**: Direction B ("Flow $\to$ Non-Flow"): recognizing when a flow/min-cut/MCMF graph has structured topology (DAG chain, complete bipartite product, tree, planar grid) that allows replacing the flow solver with **Min-Cut DP** (`CF 724 E`), **Sorted Greedy Prefix Sweep** (`ARC 125 E`), **Dynamic DP `DDP` $(\min, +)$ Matrices** (`CF 1919 F2`, `Luogu P4719`), **LGV Lemma Determinants** (`CF 348 D`), or **WQS / Aliens Lagrangian Binary Search** (`CF 739 E`, `[八省联考 2018] 林克卡特树`).
* **Prerequisite DAG**: `Ch 02`, `Ch 09`, `Ch 18`, `Ch 21`.
* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 2A` (`Iter 45`), `Wave 2B` (`Iter 46`), `Wave 3A` (`Iter 24`, `Iter 47`), `Wave 3B` (`Iter 30`, `Iter 48`), `Wave 5A` (`Iter 95`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `23.1` Min-Cut DP on Structured Graphs: Translating $x_i \in \{S, T\}$ Directly into DP States Tracking $|S \cap \{1..i\}|$ (`CF 724 E`).
  * `23.2` Closed-Form Complete Bipartite Min-Cut via Degree Sorting (`ARC 125 E — Snack`).
  * `23.3` Dynamic Min-Cut via $(\min, +)$ Tropical Matrix Multiplication on Segment Trees & HLD (`CF 1919 F2 — Wine Factory Hard`, `Luogu P4719`).
  * `23.4` Eliminating Convex $K$-Path MCMF via WQS Binary Search (`[八省联考 2018] 林克卡特树` & `CF 739 E`) and LGV Determinants (`CF 348 D`).
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: The problem is a natural max-flow, min-cut, or $K$-path MCMF, but $N \in [10^4, 3\times 10^5]$ or $Q \le 2\times 10^5$ online capacity updates makes even $O(N \log N)$ virtualized Dinic/MCMF impossible.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**:
     * **Min-Cut DP**: Dualize max-flow into $\min_{x_i \in \{0, 1\}} \text{Cut}(\mathbf{x})$; if cross-edges between $i < j$ have uniform capacity $c$, the cross-cut cost at step $i$ depends *only* on the scalar count $k = \sum_{j < i} x_j$!
     * **WQS Binary Search**: Because the MCMF value $F(k)$ as a function of flow $k$ is convex/concave (by residual path monotonicity), dualize the global cardinality constraint $|f| = K$ via Lagrangian multiplier $\lambda$: $g(\lambda) = \max_f (\text{Val}(f) - \lambda |f|)$.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**: Replace the flow solver with an $O(N^2)$ rolling DP (`CF 724 E`), an $O(N \log N)$ two-pointer sweep (`ARC 125 E`), a $2\times 2$ $(\min, +)$ matrix segment tree (`CF 1919 F2`), or an $O(N \log V)$ WQS tree DP (`林克卡特树`).
  4. **Failure Audit**: Applying WQS binary search when the underlying graph is NOT a valid flow/matroid problem (so $F(k)$ has non-convex dents) misses points inside the convex hull; collinearity ties on flat slope segments require tracking `(value, min_count)` pairs.
* **Flagship Worked Example**: **Codeforces 724 E — Goods Transportation** (`#217`, Min-Cut DP) and **Luogu P4383 — [八省联考 2018] 林克卡特树** (`#224`, Tree-MCMF convexity + WQS 3-state Tree DP).

### Chapter 24: Simulated Min-Cost Flow (`模拟费用流`), Regret Heaps (`反悔贪心`) & State-Machine Segment Trees

* **Core Structural Theme**: Simulated Min-Cost Flow (`模拟费用流`, 2019 National Team Paper by 陈江伦): exact bijection between Successive Shortest Path (`SSP`) residual augmentations and **Regret Heaps (`反悔贪心`)** (`CF 865 D`, `CF 802 N/O`), $k$-group assignment regret automata (`CF 730 I`, `AGC 018 C`), 5-candidate coupled bipartite matching automata (`[NOI2019] 序列`), Dynamic Segment Tree sign-flipping subarray MCMF (`CF 280 D`), Time-Reversed Matroid $T \to S$ Flow Decrement (`[NOI2017] 蔬菜`), and 1D/Tree Metric Spine Mergeable-Heap RLE Simulation (`[WC2019] 雪灾与外卖`, `ICPC WF 2018 Conquer the World`).

* **Source Waves Dissolved**: `Wave 3B` (`Iter 30`, `Iter 48`), `Wave 4A` (`Iter 35`), `Wave 4B` (`Iter 49`), `Wave 5A` (`Iter 42`, `Iter 49`, `Iter 92`, `Iter 93`, `Iter 95`).
* **Sub-sections & Core Derivations**:
  * `24.1` The Fundamental Bijection: Why Pushing $-v_j + 2v_i$ (or Pushing $v_i$ Twice) into a Priority Queue Materializes the Exact Residual Undo Arc of SSP.
  * `24.2` Multi-Group & Coupled Bipartite Regret Automata: 3-Heap 2-Group (`CF 730 I`), 5-Transition 2-Star (`CF 436 E`), and 5-Heap Coupled Bridge (`[NOI2019] 序列`).
  * `24.3` Dynamic Spine Segment Tree Sign-Flipping (`CF 280 D — k-Maximum Subsequence Sum`) & Time-Reversed $T \to S$ Decrement (`[NOI2017] 蔬菜`).
  * `24.4` 1D & Tree Metric Transportation (`[WC2019] 雪灾与外卖` & `Conquer the World`): $-2\text{dep}(u)$ LCA Potential Tricks, Mandatory $+\infty$ Penalties, and Amortized RLE Collapse Proofs.
* **4-Step "Zero Magic Gadgets" Compilation Contract**:
  1. **Diagnostic Signal**: A min-cost flow / matching problem on a 1D timeline, $k$-group hub, or tree metric with $N \in [10^5, 5\times 10^5]$ where explicit SSP would take $O(N^2)$ time.
  2. **Algebraic Representation**: Enumerate the finite family of minimal augmenting path types $\mathcal{P}_1, \dots, \mathcal{P}_m$ (or augmenting cycles) in the residual network $G_f$; express each path's reduced cost as a separable sum of endpoint states maintained in priority queues or segment tree nodes.
  3. **Mechanical Graph Compilation**:
     * When augmenting along $u \to v$, immediately insert the **residual undo token** into the opposite heap (with cost $-c(u, v)$) so a future node $w$ can cancel $(u, v)$ and form $(u, w)$ or $(u, x) + (y, w)$.
     * On trees (`Conquer the World`), represent $\text{dist}(u, v) = \text{dep}(u) + \text{dep}(v) - 2\text{dep}(\text{lca}(u, v))$: store $\text{dep}(u)$ in mergeable leftist heaps, match at $\text{lca}$ with offset $-2\text{dep}(\text{lca})$, and push undo tokens $-v_{\text{opt}} + 2\text{dep}(\text{lca})$.
  4. **Failure Audit**: In `CF 865 D`, pushing $p_i$ only *once* when matching $(p_j, p_i)$ forbids $p_i$ from remaining idle if a later $p_k > p_i$ steals $p_j$; pushing $p_i$ *twice* (one as the sold stock's regret token, one as day $i$'s unused buy option) is exact. In `[WC2019] 雪灾与外卖`, omitting Run-Length Encoding (`RLE`) when one mouse splits across multiple holes degrades heap size to $O(N^2)$.
