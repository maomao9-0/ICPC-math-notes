Multilingual Bibliography & Terminology Rosetta Stone

132 Primary Sources Across English [EN], Chinese [ZH], and Japanese [JA] Literature + 88-Entry Terminology Dictionary

Competitive programming flow modelling has evolved across three semi-independent traditions:

  • [EN] Western Combinatorial Optimization & Computer Vision: Formalized foundational duality (Ford–Fulkerson, Hoffman, König, Hall, Dilworth, Edmonds, Gomory–Hu), project selection (Picard 1976), and pseudo-Boolean / multi-label energy minimization in computer vision (Kolmogorov–Zabin 2004, Ishikawa 2003, Boros–Hammer 2002).
  • [ZH] Chinese Olympiad (NOI / 国家集训队论文 / 网络流 24 题): Pioneered competitive programming systematizations of Maximum Weight Closure and Maximum Density Subgraphs (Hu伯涛 2007), Consecutive Equation Differencing (流守恒差分建模法, [NOI2008] 志愿者招募), Data-Structure-Accelerated Flow Graphs (线段树/主席树优化建图), Simulated Cost Flow (模拟费用流 / 反悔贪心), and \(L_p\) Isotonic Regression (保序回归, 2018–2020).
  • [JA] Japanese AtCoder (ARC / AGC) & Kyopro Ecosystem: Systematized minimum-cut compilation as a formal algebraic calculus called 燃やす埋める (Burn-or-Bury / Project Selection), emphasizing Bipartite Variable Inversion (二部グラフの片側反転), Multi-State Ordered Chains (K値燃やす埋める), and Monge cross-difference conditions (noshi91, maspy, snuke, rng_58).

1. Foundational [EN] Academic Papers & Textbooks

  1. Ford, L. R., & Fulkerson, D. R. (1956, 1962). Maximal flow through a network (Canadian Journal of Mathematics, 8, 399–404) & Flows in Networks (Princeton University Press). — Establishes the Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem, augmenting paths, and the Integrality Theorem (Ch 01).
  2. Dinitz (Dinic), E. A. (1970) & Even, S., & Tarjan, R. E. (1975). Algorithm for solution of a problem of maximum flow in a network with power estimation (Soviet Math. Doklady, 11, 1277–1280) & Network flow and testing graph connectivity (SIAM J. Comput., 4(4), 507–518). — Proves blocking-flow level-graph bounds \(O(V^2 E)\), \(O(E \min(V^{2/3}, E^{1/2}))\) on unit-capacity networks, and \(O(E\sqrt{V})\) on unit networks (Ch 01, Ch 05).
  3. Picard, J.-C. (1976) & Picard, J.-C., & Queyranne, M. (1980). Maximal closure of a graph and applications to combinatorial problems (Management Science, 22(11), 1268–1272) & On the structure of all minimum cuts in a network and applications (Mathematical Programming Study, 13, 8–16). — Proves the Maximum Weight Closure reduction (Ch 09) and the bijection between all minimum \(s\)-\(t\) cuts and ideals of the residual SCC condensation DAG (Ch 02).
  4. Hoffman, A. J. (1960) & Hoffman, A. J., & Kruskal, J. B. (1956). Some recent applications of the theory of linear inequalities to extremal combinatorial analysis & Integral boundary points of convex polyhedra. — Establishes Hoffman’s Circulation Theorem (Ch 04) and the Totally Unimodular (TU) matrix characterization of integral polyhedra (Ch 21).
  5. König, D. (1931), Hall, P. (1935), Dilworth, R. P. (1950), & Dulmage, A. L., & Mendelsohn, N. S. (1958). Foundational duality theorems for bipartite vertex covers (Ch 05), neighborhood marriage conditions (Ch 08), poset chain decompositions (Ch 08), and canonical bipartite vertex/edge partitions (Ch 06).
  6. Birkhoff, G. (1946) & von Neumann, J. (1953). Decomposition of doubly stochastic and \(k\)-regular bipartite matrices into permutation matchings (Ch 07).
  7. Kolmogorov, V., & Zabin, R. (2004). What energy functions can be minimized via graph cuts? (IEEE TPAMI, 26(2), 147–159). — Proves that a pairwise pseudo-Boolean function is graph-representable if and only if every pairwise term satisfies submodularity \(E(0,0) + E(1,1) \le E(0,1) + E(1,0)\) (Ch 10).
  8. Boros, E., & Hammer, P. L. (2002). Pseudo-Boolean optimization (Discrete Applied Mathematics, 123, 155–225). — Establishes Quadratic Pseudo-Boolean Optimization (QPBO), roof duality, half-integrality, and bipartite variable flipping (Ch 11).
  9. Ishikawa, H. (2003). Exact optimization for Markov random fields with convex priors (IEEE TPAMI, 25(10), 1333–1336). — Introduces multi-state ordered chains with reverse \(\infty\)-arcs and cross-difference capacities \(-\Delta_a \Delta_b \Phi(a,b) \ge 0\) (Ch 12, Ch 13).
  10. Chambolle, A. (2004) & Darbon, J., & Sigelle, M. (2006). * Exact total variation minimization and the discrete coarea formula via parametric level-set minimum cuts* (Ch 13, CF 1427 G).
  11. Dinkelbach, W. (1967) & Goldberg, A. V. (1984). On nonlinear fractional programming (Management Science, 13(7), 492–498) & Finding a maximum density subgraph (UC Berkeley Tech Report UCB/CSD-84-171) (Ch 14).
  12. Hassin, R. (1981), Reif, J. H. (1983), Stoer, M., & Wagner, F. (1997), Gomory, R. E., & Hu, T. C. (1961), & Gusfield, D. (1990). Planar \(s\)-\(t\) cycle-cut duality (Ch 15), global minimum cut, and \(N-1\) all-pairs min-cut tree construction (Ch 16).
  13. Edmonds, J., & Karp, R. M. (1972) & Suurballe, J. W. (1974). Johnson vertex potentials \(\pi(v)\) for non-negative reduced costs in MCMF and edge-disjoint shortest paths (Ch 17, Ch 20).
  14. Edmonds, J. (1965, 1970) & Tutte, W. T. (1947, 1954). Paths, trees, and flowers (Blossom contraction and Tutte–Berge duality, Ch 22), The factorization of linear graphs (Tutte skew-symmetric matrix and \(f\)-factors, Ch 22), and Submodular functions, matroids, and certain polyhedra (Ch 21, Ch 24).
  15. Ahuja, R. K., Magnanti, T. L., & Orlin, J. B. (1993) & Schrijver, A. (2003). Network Flows: Theory, Algorithms, and Applications (Prentice Hall) & Combinatorial Optimization: Polyhedra and Efficiency (3 vols., Springer). — Comprehensive reference monographs across Parts I–VI.

2. Landmark [ZH] Chinese Competitive Programming Treatises (国家集训队论文 & Classics)

  1. Hu Botao (胡伯涛, 2007). 《最小割模型在信息学竞赛中的应用》 (Applications of Minimum Cut Models in Olympiad in Informatics), IOI 2007 Chinese National Training Team Paper. — The seminal Chinese CP monograph introducing Picard’s Maximum Weight Closure (最大权闭合子图, Ch 09) and Goldberg/Amber’s Maximum Density Subgraph (最大密度子图, Ch 14), with canonical proofs of vertex-only degree-sum reductions.
  2. Zhou Dong (周冬, 2009). 《浅析最大最小定理在信息学竞赛中的应用》 (Applications of Min-Max Theorems in OI), IOI 2009 Chinese National Training Team Paper. — Systematizes planar cycle-cut duality (平面图最小割转对偶图最短路, Ch 15), König’s theorem, and Dilworth’s theorem.
  3. Jiangind / NOI 2008 Committee ([NOI2008] 志愿者招募, 2008) & Subsequent National Team Treatises on LP Duality (LP对偶转网络流). — Introduces the Algebraic Equation-Difference Method (流守恒差分建模法) (Ch 19) and explicit primal–dual LP compilation (Ch 21).
  4. Zkw (张昆玮, Tsinghua University, 2009). 《统计的力量 — 费用流与zkw算法》. — Introduces the primal–dual multi-augmenting-path MCMF algorithm (zkw费用流) and analyzes its performance on dense unit-length networks vs. long zero-cost paths (Ch 17).
  5. Xu Haoran / National Team Papers on Simulated Cost Flow (2017–2019). 《模拟费用流问题综述》 (A Survey of Simulated Min-Cost Flow Problems). — Formalizes the bijection between Successive Shortest Path augmenting paths and priority-queue regret greedy (反悔贪心), 1D mouse-to-hole (UOJ #455), tree LCA merge, and 5-queue coupled bipartite state machines ([NOI2019] 序列, Ch 23).
  6. Gou Zihao (2018–2020) & [省选联考 2020] 魔法商店. 《保序回归问题浅谈》 (An Introduction to Isotonic Regression in OI). — Establishes the parallel binary search (整体二分) + closure min-cut reduction for \(L_p\) isotonic regression (Ch 24).
  7. OI-Wiki Contributors. Network Flow (网络流), Minimum Cut (最小割), Min-Cost Max-Flow (费用流), Bounded Flows (上下界网络流), Blossom (带花树), and 网络流 24 题 Reference Articles.

3. Landmark [JA] Japanese Competitive Programming Treatises (AtCoder / Kyopro)

  1. noshi91 (2019–2021). 「燃やす埋める問題（Project Selection Problem）の解説」 (Comprehensive Guide to the Burn-or-Bury / Project Selection Problem). — Formalizes the 2-variable submodularity table, Bipartite Variable Inversion (二部グラフの片側反転), and multi-state Monge conditions (K値燃やす埋める, Ch 10–Ch 13).
  2. maspy (2020–2023). 「最小カットと燃やす埋める」 & Editorial Series on AtCoder Regular/Grand Contests. — Detailed algebraic derivations of pseudo-Boolean cut polynomials, Hall’s Marriage Theorem flow eliminations, and LP duality (Ch 08, Ch 10–Ch 13, Ch 21).
  3. snuke & rng_58 (AtCoder Official Editorials, 2016–2022). — Canonical problem designs and editorials for AGC 038 F (Two Permutations), ARC 107 F (Sum of Abs), ARC 142 E (Pairing Wizards), AGC 031 E (Snuke the Phantom Thief), AGC 034 D (Manhattan Max Matching), AGC 037 D (Sorting a Grid), ARC 076 F, and ARC 085 E.
