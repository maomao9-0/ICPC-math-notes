06 · Practice — Polynomial Algorithms

These exercises begin where a tested polynomial library ends: each requires a modelling step or a non-obvious use of polynomial machinery.

Solved exercises

A. Samples are not a degree bound

Recover a polynomial of degree at most two from values 1, 6, 17 at 0, 1, 2. Give a different polynomial with the same samples when the bound is removed.

TipHint

The second finite difference determines twice the quadratic coefficient.

NoteSolution

Writing \(f=a+bx+cx^2\) gives \(a=1\), \(b+c=5\), \(2b+4c=16\), hence \(c=3,b=2\). Thus \(f=1+2x+3x^2\). The polynomial \(f+x(x-1)(x-2)\) has the same samples but degree three. These computations require rational arithmetic or a field in which 2 is a unit; in characteristic two the sample points 0 and 2 coincide.

B. A false CRT shortcut

An integer convolution coefficient is known only to lie in \([0,100]\). Can its residue modulo 17 determine its residue modulo 10?

TipHint

Find two bounded numbers agreeing modulo 17.

NoteSolution

No: 1 and 18 agree modulo 17 but differ modulo 10. Auxiliary CRT moduli must have product greater than 100 for unambiguous reconstruction on this interval. Garner’s algorithm changes representation, not this information limit.

C. Sum of pair differences

Two multisets contain nonnegative integers at most \(D\). Derive all counts of ordered pairs \((a,b)\) with difference \(a-b=d\) for \(-D\le d\le D\).

TipHint

Reverse the second frequency array and solve its convolution index equation.

NoteSolution

Let \(u_i\) count first-multiset values \(i\) and \(v_{D-j}\) count second-multiset values \(j\). Index \(i+D-j=D+d\) counts exactly the desired pairs, so answer \(d\) is \((u*v)_{D+d}\). The range 0…2D includes every difference. Complexity is \(O(M(D+1))\) after frequency construction; multiply occurrence counts, not mere presence flags. Diagonal pairs are included when the multisets coincide unless the problem explicitly excludes reusing one occurrence.
