Textbook III · Competitive Programming Mathematics (00–20)

Enter the Competitive Programming Mathematics Textbook → · Study Plan (00–20) · Notation & Contracts · Sources & Policy · Download Offline HTML (.zip)

A broad 21-Chapter foundation and synthesis curriculum covering the general mathematical pillars of competitive programming from first principles:

  • Part I · Arithmetic and counting (00–05): Foundations and proof, modular algebra, multiplicative number theory, combinatorics, binomials modulo anything, and group actions (Burnside/Pólya).
  • Part II · Algebraic engines (06–11): Polynomials and FFT/NTT, linear algebra, subset and divisor transforms, formal power series overview, generating functions overview, and set power series.
  • Part III · Models and synthesis (12–15): Probability and Markov chains, discrete sums and floor geometry, summatory sieves, and mixed capstones.
  • Part IV · Frontier topics (16–20): \(q\)-analogues and subspaces, finite-field polynomials, algebraic graph methods (Pfaffians/Tutte), holonomic methods, and frontier capstones.
  • Start here: Mathematics Overview or directly at 00 · Mathematical foundations and proof.
