## 2. Detailed Item-by-Item Mathematical & Algorithmic Audit

### 2.1 Blumer–Crochemore Duality $\mathcal{T}_{\text{link}}(\operatorname{SAM}(S)) \cong \operatorname{ST}(S^R)$ (`Ch 09, §1.2–1.3`)
- **Verification**:
  - `Lemma 9.1` proves that $\operatorname{endpos}_S(u) \cap \operatorname{endpos}_S(v) \neq \emptyset$ ($|u| \le |v|$) forces $u \sqsubseteq_{\text{suf}} v$ and $\operatorname{endpos}_S(v) \subseteq \operatorname{endpos}_S(u)$, and establishes the exact contiguous suffix-length interval $[\operatorname{len}(\operatorname{link}(x)) + 1, \operatorname{len}(x)]$ for every state $x > 1$.
  - `Theorem 9.2` establishes the reflection bijection $l' = N - r + 1$ mapping $\operatorname{endpos}_S(w)$ to $\operatorname{startpos}_{S^R}(w^R)$, showing that left-extensions $w \to cw$ in $S$ correspond to right-extensions $w^R \to w^R c$ in $S^R$, and therefore $\operatorname{LCP}(S[i..N], S[j..N]) = \operatorname{len}\bigl(\operatorname{LCA}_{\mathcal{T}_{\text{link}}(\operatorname{SAM}(S^R))}(v_i, v_j)\bigr)$ where $v_i = \operatorname{pos}_{S^R}[N - i + 1]$.
  - The dual Mermaid diagrams and 6-row correspondence table on $S = \texttt{abaab}$ and $S^R = \texttt{baaba}$ were hand-checked entry by entry.

### 2.2 Online GSAM Zero-Length Clone Corruption & $\Theta(V^2)$ Trie-DFS vs. $O(V\sigma)$ Trie-BFS (`Ch 09, §2–3`)
- **Verification**:
  - `Counterexample 9.3` traces $\mathcal{S} = \{S_1 = \texttt{ab}, S_2 = \texttt{b}\}$ under naive `last = 1` reset + single-string `extend('b')`, showing that state $4$ (`cur`) is created with $\operatorname{len}(4) = 1$, $\operatorname{link}(4) = 5$ ($\operatorname{len}(5) = 1$), empty length interval $[2, 1] = \emptyset$, DAG in-degree $0$, and stolen prefix marker $\operatorname{cnt}_{S_2}[4] = 1$.
  - The 3-case `extend_gsam(p, c)` contract (`Contract A`) inspects $q = \delta(p, c)$ prior to allocation: Case 1 ($q = 0$, standard extension), Case 2 ($\operatorname{len}(q) = \operatorname{len}(p) + 1$, return $q$), and Case 3 ($\operatorname{len}(q) > \operatorname{len}(p) + 1$, allocate **only `clone`** of length $\operatorname{len}(p) + 1$ and return `clone`).
  - `Theorem 9.4` proves the $\Theta(V^2)$ lower bound for Trie-DFS on the binary-alphabet Trie $\mathcal{T}_m = \{\texttt{a}^k\texttt{b} \mid 1 \le k \le m\}$ ($V = 2m + 1$), where visiting the `'a'`-spine first and backtracking $k = m-1 \downarrow 1$ triggers Case 3 at every leaf edge $v_k \xrightarrow{\texttt{b}} u_k$ across $k + 1$ states ($\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}(k+1) = \frac{m(m+1)}{2} - 1 = \Theta(V^2)$), and proves why Trie-BFS restores strict $O(V\sigma)$ time and $\le 2V$ states.

### 2.3 Substring Containment Clipping $\operatorname{endpos}_S(u) \cap [l + L - 1, r] \neq \emptyset$ & `CF 700E` Top-Len Sufficiency (`Ch 10, §1–3`)
- **Verification**:
  - `Theorem 10.1` proves that an occurrence of $P \in E(u)$ ($|P| = L$) ending at $e \in \operatorname{endpos}_S(u)$ starts at $e - L + 1 \ge l \iff e \ge l + L - 1$, yielding $\operatorname{occ}(P, S[l..r]) = |\operatorname{endpos}_S(u) \cap [l + L - 1, r]|$. `Counterexample 10.1` ($S = \texttt{ababa}, [l, r] = [3, 5], P = \texttt{baba}$) demonstrates the left-boundary straddling failure of unclipped $[l, r]$.
  - `Theorem 10.2` proves why online queries on interior SAM states require allocating a fresh Segment Tree node `z = ++seg_sz` whenever `x != 0 && y != 0` during `merge_seg(x, y, l, r)`, and bounds total nodes by $\le 2N(\lceil \log_2 N \rceil + 1)$.
  - `Counterexample 10.2` and `Contract B` (`compute_clipped_lim`) resolve the intra-state length-skipping trap in `[NOI2018] 你的名字` by decrementing `--L` one unit at a time and stepping `u = link[u]` only when `L == len[link[u]]`.
  - `Theorem 10.4` (`CF 700E`) proves both intra-state coalescing to $\operatorname{longest}(u)$ and the single-witness check $\operatorname{endpos}_S(v) \cap [e_0 - \operatorname{len}(u) + \operatorname{len}(v), e_0 - 1] \neq \emptyset$ for $v = \operatorname{top}[\operatorname{link}(u)]$ and any single $e_0 = \operatorname{any\_pos}[u] \in \operatorname{endpos}_S(u)$.
