## 2. Detailed Proof Verification Log

### 2.1 Fine–Wilf Periodicity Theorem & Sub-Threshold Orbit Formula (`Ch 01`)
- **Residue Decimation (`§2.2`)**: Partitions $\{1, \dots, N\}$ into $g = \gcd(p, q)$ subsequences $S^{(r)}$ of length $m_r = \lfloor (N - r)/g \rfloor + 1 \ge p' + q' - 1$ for $N \ge g(p' + q' - 1) = p + q - g$. Verified exact.
- **Directed Hamiltonian Path on $\mathbb{Z}/(p+q)\mathbb{Z}$ (`§2.3 Proof I`)**: Transition $f(i) = i + p$ ($1 \le i \le q-1$) and $f(i) = i - q$ ($q + 1 \le i \le p + q - 1$) equals $+p \pmod{p+q}$; deleting residue $0$ from the single cycle of length $p + q$ leaves the Hamiltonian path $p \to \dots \to q$ on $\{1, \dots, p + q - 1\}$. Verified exact.
- **First-Return Map $\psi$ on $\mathbb{Z}_q$ & Sub-Threshold Orbits (`§2.3 Proof II` & `§3.1 Theorem 1.3`)**: On $U = \{1, \dots, q\}$, $\psi(x) = ((x + p - 1) \bmod q) + 1$ has $g$ cycles of length $q / g$. For $q \le N < p + q - g$, the $k = p + q - N > g$ vertices $\{N - p + 1, \dots, q\}$ lose their outgoing edges. Because $k > g$ consecutive integers hit every residue class modulo $g$ at least once, all $g$ cycles break into a forest on $q$ vertices with $N - p$ active edges, leaving $q - (N - p) = p + q - N$ connected components. Verified exact.

### 2.2 Weak Periodicity, $\frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{1}{2}$ Length Drops, and Shortcut Link Duality (`Ch 02`)
- **Sharp $\frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{1}{2}$ Drop (`Theorem 2.3`)**: At a series-link transition $d_2 = \operatorname{diff}[v] < d_1 = \operatorname{diff}[u]$, the prefix and suffix copies of $v = \operatorname{fail}[u]$ inside $u$ overlap on length $\operatorname{len}[v] - d_1$. If $\operatorname{len}[v] - d_1 \ge d_2$, period $d_2$ of both copies of $v$ bridges across the overlap and forces $d_2 \in \operatorname{Per}(u)$, contradicting $d_1 = \operatorname{per}(u) > d_2$. Thus $\operatorname{len}[v] < d_1 + d_2 < 2d_1 \implies \operatorname{len}[v] < \frac{2}{3}\operatorname{len}[u]$ and $\operatorname{len}[w] = \operatorname{len}[v] - d_2 < d_1 < \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{len}[u]$. Verified exact.
- **Next-Character Equality (`Theorem 2.4`)**: When $\operatorname{diff}[u] = \operatorname{diff}[\pi[u]] = d$, both $w + 1 = u - 2d + 1 \ge 1$ and $v + 1 = u - d + 1 \le u$ lie inside $S[1..u]$ of period $d$, forcing $S[\pi[\pi[u]] + 1] = S[\pi[u] + 1]$. Verified exact.

### 2.3 `[WC2016]` Dynamic Modulus Switching & Residue-Cycle Monotonic Queue (`Ch 03`)
- **Modulus Switch ($m_{\text{old}} \to x_k$, `§2.3`)**: Seeds $D_{\text{new}}[D_{\text{old}}[r] \bmod x_k] \leftarrow \min(\dots, D_{\text{old}}[r])$ and unrolls each of the $\gcd(m_{\text{old}}, x_k)$ cycles of step $+m_{\text{old}} \pmod{x_k}$ for $x_k / \gcd(m_{\text{old}}, x_k) - 1$ steps starting from $u_{\min} = \arg\min_{u \in C} D_{\text{new}}[u]$. Verified exact.
- **Residue-Cycle Monotonic Queue (`§3.1–3.3`)**: Unrolls each of the $\gcd(d_k, x_k)$ cycles of step $+d_k \pmod{x_k}$ from $v_0 = \arg\min_{v \in C} D[v]$, relaxing $D[v_t] \leftarrow \min(D[v_t], x_k + t d_k + \min_{t - c_k \le s \le t - 1} (D[v_s] - s d_k))$ before pushing the updated $D[v_t] - t d_k$ into the deque. Verified exact.

### 2.4 `slink` DP Shift-Overlap Invariant (`Ch 04`)
- **Shift-Overlap Invariant (`Theorem 4.2`)**: Proves (1) $\mathcal{I}(u, r) = \mathcal{I}(\operatorname{fail}[u], r - d) \sqcup \{r - \operatorname{len}[\operatorname{slink}[u]] - d\}$, (2) $v = \operatorname{fail}[u]$ was a `slink` chain head at step $r - d$ (otherwise its period-$d$ child $w$ at $r - d$ would equal $u$ and extend $u$ at $r$ to $u^+$ with $\operatorname{diff}[u^+] = d$, contradicting that $u$ is a chain head at $r$), and (3) $v$ never occurred in $(r - d, r)$ (otherwise $u$ would have a period $\gcd(d, r - r'') < d$ by Weak Periodicity). Verified exact.

### 2.5 Chen–Fox–Lyndon Uniqueness & Duval's Potential Function (`Ch 05`)
- **CFL Uniqueness (`Theorem 5.2`)**: Proves existence via adjacent merges $w_i < w_{i+1} \implies w_i w_{i+1} \in \mathcal{L}$ and uniqueness via first-factor contradiction $w_1 < p \le w'_r \le \dots \le w'_1 \sqsubset w_1$. Verified exact.
- **Duval's Potential Function (`Theorem 5.4`)**: $\Phi(i, k) = 2i + k$ increases by $+1$ in Cases 1 & 2, and by $\Delta \Phi = 2 d p + (1 - |w'|) \ge d p + 2 \ge 3 > 0$ in Case 3 since $|w'| \le p - 1 \le d p - 1$. Verified exact.

### 2.6 Jin Ce's Significant Suffixes & Segment Tree `pushup` 1-Survivor Theorem (`Ch 06`)
- **Tandem-Annihilation & Doubling (`Theorem 6.2`)**: For $u, v \in \operatorname{Suf}(U)$ with $|u| < |v| < 2|u|$ and $u \sqsubset v$, sets $p = |v| - |u| < |u|$, $w = v[1..p]$, $u' = u[p+1..|u|] \in \operatorname{Suf}(U)$ so $u = w u'$ and $v = w^2 u'$. By Left-Cancellation Invariance, $u'T < uT = w u' T \implies w u' T < w^2 u' T = vT$ and $u'T > uT \implies uT > vT$, proving $\min(u'T, vT) < uT < \max(u'T, vT)$ for all $T \in \Sigma^*$. Verified exact.
- **Segment Tree `pushup` 1-Survivor (`Theorem 6.3`)**: At midpoint $M = \lfloor (L + R)/2 \rfloor$, all suffixes starting in $[L, M]$ have lengths in $[R - M + 1, R - L + 1]$ at right boundary $R$, with ratio $\frac{R - L + 1}{R - M + 1} < 2$. By Theorem 6.2(3), at most one candidate from $\operatorname{Cand}([L, M])$ can survive in $\operatorname{Cand}([L, R])$, yielding $O(\log(R - L + 1))$ LCE calls per node and $\sum_{h \ge 1} \frac{N}{2^h} O(h) = O(N)$ total build time. Verified exact.
