08 — Generating Functions and Coefficient Extraction

A generating function (GF) is a modelling language; an FPS is the algebra used to compute it. The critical skill is translating combinatorial construction into an equation before choosing an algorithm.

1. OGF or EGF?

An ordinary generating function (OGF) stores (A(x)=a_nx^n). Its product splits an integer size: ([x^n]AB=ia_i b{n-i}). Use it for sequences, weights, paths and otherwise unlabelled atoms.

An exponential generating function (EGF) stores (A(x)=a_nx^n/n!). Its product distributes a set of labels:

[ n![x^n]AB=i{ni}a_i b{n-i}. ]

Use it when labels are partitioned among components. A useful labelled-class dictionary is:

construction EGF
disjoint choice (A+B)
labelled product (AB)
sequence of A (1/(1-A))
set of A components ((A))
cycle of A components ((1/(1-A)))

The last three require A(0)=0. Examples: permutations are sets of cycles, so their EGF is (((1/(1-x)))=1/(1-x)). Set partitions are sets of nonempty sets: ((e^x-1)).

2. Recurrences become algebra

If (a_n={i=1}^d c_i a{n-i}), multiply by x^n and sum:

[ A(x)=,Q(x)=1-_{i=1}dc_ixi, ]

where P = (initial-prefix * Q) mod x^d. Conversely, a rational OGF gives a constant-coefficient linear recurrence. This equivalence lets you choose among:

  • DP: (O(nd)), good for small n;
  • polynomial inverse: first n terms in (O(M(n)));
  • Kitamasa: one huge term in (O(d^2n));
  • Bostan–Mori: one huge term in (O(M(d)n)).

3. Bostan–Mori

To find ([x^k]P(x)/Q(x)), multiply top and bottom by (Q(-x)). The denominator (Q(x)Q(-x)) contains only even powers. Keep the numerator powers matching the parity of k, discard the other parity, replace (x^2) by x, and halve k.

Normalize Q[0] != 0, reduce P mod Q initially if necessary, and trim degrees after multiplications. Complexity is (O(M(d)k)).

4. Functional equations

Recursive objects often give implicit equations. For plane rooted trees whose root has an ordered sequence of subtrees, (T=x(1+T+T^2+)=x/(1-T)). Binary trees with allowed node-weight inventory W(x) give (F=1+WF^2). Solve algebraically only after specifying the empty object and whether children are ordered.

Some equations can be evaluated with FPS inv/sqrt/exp; others are better used directly for coefficient extraction by Lagrange inversion.

5. Lagrange–Bürmann inversion

If (T(x)=x(T(x))) with ((0)), then for (n>0)

[ [x^n]T(x)k=[t{n-k}](t)^n, ]

and more generally

[ [x^n]H(T(x))=1n[t^{n-1}]H’(t)(t)^n. ]

Proof sketch via residues

Coefficient extraction is a formal residue: ([x^n]H(T)=_x H(T)x^{-n-1}). Substitute x=t/Phi(t); the formal change-of-variables rule supplies dx=(Phi-t Phi')/Phi^2 dt. Expanding and integrating the formal derivative term by parts leaves (1n[t^{n-1}]H’(t)(t)^n). No analytic convergence is involved.

Worked example. Labelled rooted trees satisfy T=x exp(T). Then ([x^n]T=1n[t^{n-1}]e{nt}=n{n-1}/n!); multiplying by n! gives Cayley’s (n^{n-1}) rooted labelled trees.

Recognition signal: the unknown appears recursively on both sides, but can be rearranged to T=x Phi(T). If the target is one coefficient, do not compute the whole compositional inverse.

6. Coefficient techniques beyond dense FPS

Diagonal / constant term

Constraints such as i=j can be enforced by an auxiliary variable: (i a{i,i}x^i=_z A(x/z,z)). Roots-of-unity filters select indices modulo m: over a field containing an mth root w,

[ {nrm}a_nx^n= 1m{j=0}{m-1}w{-rj}A(w^jx). ]

Logarithmic derivative

Products over many factor types become sums: ((F)‘=F’/F). For (F=k(1-xk){-c_k}), [ [x^{n-1}]F’/F={dn} d c_d. ] Recover F with FPS exponential or a coefficient recurrence. This is central to partition and multiset enumeration.

Differential equations

An algebraic or exponential GF often satisfies A'(x)=B(x)A(x) or another coefficient-friendly differential equation. With sparse B, the induced recurrence can beat a general FPS routine.

7. Modulus and modelling pitfalls

  • EGF coefficients contain inverse factorials: usually require n < MOD.
  • Formal division requires an invertible constant denominator.
  • T=x Phi(T) needs Phi(0) != 0 for a unique series with T(0)=0.
  • Ordered sequence, unordered set and cycle constructions are different.
  • A closed algebraic expression may introduce a branch; select the constant term dictated by the combinatorial class.
  • A huge requested index plus small rational denominator signals Bostan–Mori, not expansion through that index.
