08 · Practice — Generating Functions

1. AtCoder ABC 422 G — Balls and Boxes (Foundation)

  • Statement · official editorial · English/Japanese
  • Train: decide OGF versus EGF from distinguishability and translate products.
Hint 1 Write a generating function for the choices contributed by one box.
Hint 2 Ask whether assigning labels to two groups introduces a binomial coefficient.
Hint 3 Factorial-scale EGF coefficients so the remaining operations become ordinary convolutions.

2. AtCoder ABC 300 Ex — Fibonacci: Revisited (Advanced)

  • Statement · official editorial · Japanese (formulas/code are language-independent)
  • Train: construct a rational OGF for a recurrence and extract a huge coefficient with Bostan–Mori.
Hint 1 Encode the order-d recurrence as a denominator polynomial.
Hint 2 Multiply by Q(-x) so the new denominator has only even powers.
Hint 3 Keep numerator coefficients with the parity of the requested index, then halve the index.

3. Codeforces 438E — The Child and Binary Tree (Mastery)

  • Statement · official tutorial · English/Russian · CF 3100
  • Train: derive an algebraic GF from recursive weighted trees; FPS square root and inverse.
Hint 1 Let W mark the permitted weight of the root and F include the empty tree.
Hint 2 An ordered pair of child trees contributes F^2, hence F=1+W F^2.
Hint 3 Select the quadratic-formula branch with constant coefficient one.

4. Codeforces 960G — Bandit Blues (Mastery)

  • Statement · official tutorial · English/Russian · CF 2900
  • Train: permutation records, unsigned Stirling numbers, factorial-scaled convolution.
Hint 1 Condition on the position/value of the global maximum.
Hint 2 Left-to-right maxima and cycles of a permutation share the same Stirling distribution.
Hint 3 The final sum over a split point is a convolution after factorial scaling/reversal.

5. Codeforces 1342F — Make It Ascending (Capstone)

  • Statement · official tutorial · English/Russian · CF 3000
  • Train: derive an implicit GF and apply Lagrange inversion together with FFT.
Hint 1 Group histories by the component containing a distinguished element.
Hint 2 Express the recursive component class as a functional equation T=x Phi(T).
Hint 3 Use Lagrange–Bürmann to turn the target coefficient into a coefficient of a power, then batch the resulting sums by convolution.
