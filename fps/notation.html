FPS Notation, Operators & 16 Failure Modes

Canonical ring definitions, operator contracts, and defensive algebraic checks

The 24-chapter Formal Power Series & Generating Functions textbook enforces the following canonical ring, operator, and defensive-contract notation across all chapters, checkpoints, and capstones.

Base Ring & Coefficient Conventions

  • \(\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\) denotes the nonnegative integers; \([0, n)\) denotes integer indices \(0, \dots, n-1\). Empty sums are \(0\), empty products are \(1\), and the unique empty combinatorial object has size \(0\) and contributes \(1 \cdot x^0\).
  • \([P]\) is the Iverson indicator of proposition \(P\) (\(1\) if \(P\) holds, \(0\) otherwise); this is distinct from the linear coefficient-extraction functional \([x^n]F(x)\).
  • \(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}\) is the ring of residues modulo \(m\); \(\mathbb{F}_p\) is a prime field of characteristic \(p\); \(\mathbb{F}_q\) denotes a finite field of prime-power order \(q = p^r\).
  • Division by \(a \in K\) means multiplication by \(a^{-1}\) and requires \(a \in K^\times\) (a unit in \(K\)). Over \(\mathbb{F}_p\), \(a \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times \iff a \not\equiv 0 \pmod p\); over \(\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}\), \(a \in (\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z})^\times \iff \gcd(a, m) = 1\).
  • For nonzero \(n \in \mathbb{Z}\), \(v_p(n)\) denotes the \(p\)-adic exponent of prime \(p\) dividing \(n\), with \(v_p(0) = +\infty\).
  • \(\mathcal{M}(N)\) (or \(M(N)\)) denotes the arithmetic cost of multiplying two polynomials of degree \(< N\); over an NTT-friendly prime modulus, \(\mathcal{M}(N) = O(N \log N)\).

Rings, Valuations, and Formal Laurent Residues

  • Formal Power Series Ring \(K[[x]]\): For a commutative ring \(K\) with unity, \(K[[x]]\) is the ring of infinite formal series \(F(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} f_n x^n\) equipped with Cauchy convolution \([x^n](F \cdot G) = \sum_{i+j=n} f_i g_j\).
  • \(x\)-Adic Valuation \(\nu(F) = \operatorname{val}(F) = \operatorname{ord}_x(F)\): The valuation (or order, also written \(\operatorname{val}_x(F)\) or \(\operatorname{ord}_x(F)\)) of \(F(x) \in K((x))\) is the lowest index of a non-zero coefficient: \[\nu(F) = \operatorname{val}(F) = \operatorname{ord}_x(F) = \min\{n \in \mathbb{Z} : [x^n]F(x) \neq 0\}, \qquad \nu(0) = +\infty.\] An infinite sum \(\sum_{k \ge 0} F_k(x)\) or infinite product \(\prod_{k \ge 1}(1 + G_k(x))\) converges in the \(x\)-adic topology if and only if \(\lim_{k \to \infty} \nu(F_k) = +\infty\) (respectively \(\lim_{k \to \infty} \nu(G_k) = +\infty\)).
  • Unit Criterion in \(K[[x]]^\times\): \(F(x) \in K[[x]]\) has a multiplicative inverse \(F(x)^{-1} \in K[[x]]\) if and only if its constant term is a unit in \(K\): \([x^0]F(x) \in K^\times\).
  • Formal Laurent Series Ring \(K((x))\): \(K((x)) = K[[x]][x^{-1}]\) consists of formal series \(A(x) = \sum_{n=v}^{\infty} a_n x^n\) with finite lower bound \(v \in \mathbb{Z}\) (at most finitely many negative powers of \(x\)). When \(K\) is a field, \(K((x))\) is the fraction field of \(K[[x]]\).
  • Formal Residue Operator \(\operatorname{Res}_{x=0} = \operatorname{Res}_x\): For \(F(x) \in K((x))\), the formal residue is purely algebraic: \[\operatorname{Res}_{x=0} F(x) = \operatorname{Res}_x F(x) = \operatorname{Res}_{x=0} F(x)\,dx = [x^{-1}]F(x).\] It satisfies \(\operatorname{Res}_{x=0} A'(x) = 0\) for all \(A(x) \in K((x))\) in every characteristic, and Jacobi’s Formal Change-of-Variables Formula for any \(G(u) \in K[[u]]\) with \(\nu(G) = 1\) and \([u^1]G(u) \in K^\times\): \[\operatorname{Res}_{x=0} F(x)\,dx = \operatorname{Res}_{u=0} F(G(u))\,G'(u)\,du.\]
  • 5-Ring Bivariate Hierarchy (Ch 15–Ch 17):
    1. \(K[y][[x]]\) — polynomials in \(y\) over \(x\)-power series (\(\deg_y f_n(y) < \infty\) for each \(n\));
    2. \((K[y, y^{-1}])[[x]]\) — Laurent polynomials in \(y\) at each power of \(x\) (the natural ring for 1D lattice walk step polynomials, where substituting a small root \(y = y_0(x)\) with \(\nu_x(y_0) \ge 1\) converges in the \(x\)-adic topology);
    3. \(K[[x, y]]\) — formal power series in two commuting variables (supporting arbitrary \((n, m) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^2\));
    4. \(K((x))[[y]]\) — formal power series in \(y\) over the Laurent field \(K((x))\) (bounded below in \(x\) for each fixed \(y^m\); note that \(K((x))[[y]]\) and \(K((y))[[x]]\) are not isomorphic: \(\frac{1}{x - y} = \sum_{k \ge 0} x^{-k-1} y^k \in K((x))[[y]]\) vs. \(-\sum_{k \ge 0} x^k y^{-k-1} \in K((y))[[x]]\));
    5. \(K((z))[[t]]\) — formal power series in \(t\) over the Laurent field \(K((z))\) (the Furstenberg Diagonal Ring, where \(x = z, \, y = t z^{-1}\) converts \([x^n y^n]F(x, y)\) into the 1D residue \([t^n]\operatorname{Res}_{z=0}\bigl(z^{-1}F(z, t z^{-1})\bigr)\) because each fixed power \(t^k\) has Laurent \(z\)-valuation bounded below by \(-k\)).

Hadamard Products, Diagonals, Differential & Transposed Operators

  • Hadamard Product \(F \odot G\): Termwise (coefficient-wise) product of two univariate series: \[(F \odot G)(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \bigl([x^n]F(x)\bigr)\bigl([x^n]G(x)\bigr) x^n.\]
  • Constant-Term & Diagonal Operator \(\operatorname{Diag}_{x,y}\): For \(F(x,y) = \sum_{i,j \ge 0} f_{i,j} x^i y^j\), the main diagonal is extracted via the Laurent substitution \(x = u, y = t/u\) in \(K[[t]]((u))\): \[\operatorname{Diag}_{x,y} F(x,y) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} f_{n,n} t^n = [u^0] F(u, t/u) = \operatorname{Res}_{u=0} \frac{1}{u} F(u, t/u).\]
  • Euler Differential Operator \(\vartheta\) (and Pointing \(\Theta\), \(\vartheta_x = x D_x\), \(\Theta_x = x \partial_x\)): \[\vartheta = \Theta = x \frac{d}{dx}, \qquad \vartheta^k\!\left(\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} f_n x^n\right) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} n^k f_n x^n = \sum_{j=0}^{k} \left\{\begin{matrix} k \\ j \end{matrix}\right\} x^j F^{(j)}(x).\]
  • Weyl Algebra \(K[x]\langle D \rangle\) & Shift Algebra \(K[n]\langle E_n \rangle\): Non-commutative operator rings governed by the Leibniz commutation relations: \[D x - x D = 1 \quad \text{in } K[x]\langle D \rangle, \qquad E_n n - (n + 1) E_n = 0 \quad \text{in } K[n]\langle E_n \rangle,\] connected on coefficients via \([x^n]\bigl(x^a D^b F(x)\bigr) = (n - a + 1)^{\overline{b}} f_{n - a + b}\).
  • Tellegen Transposed Multiplication (Middle Product mulT / \(\operatorname{mulT}(C, B)\) / \(\operatorname{mul}^T\)): Given \(B(x)\) of degree \(< m\) and \(C(x)\) of degree \(< m + n - 1\), the transposed multiplication \(\operatorname{mulT}(C, B) = T_B^\top \mathbf{c}\) (also written \(\operatorname{mul}^T(B, C)\)) is the linear transpose of \(A \mapsto A \cdot B\) on the dual space \(K[x]_{<n+m-1}^* \to K[x]_{<n}^*\): \[[x^i]\operatorname{mulT}(C, B) = \sum_{j=0}^{m-1} b_j c_{i+j} = [x^i]\bigl(B(x^{-1}) C(x)\bigr) \qquad (0 \le i < n).\]
  • \(q\)-Pochhammer Symbol & Gaussian \(q\)-Binomial Coefficient: \[(a; q)_n = \prod_{i=0}^{n-1} (1 - a q^i), \qquad (a; q)_0 = 1, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} n \\ k \end{bmatrix}_q = \binom{n}{k}_q = \frac{(q; q)_n}{(q; q)_k (q; q)_{n-k}}.\]
  • Set Power Series Ring \(\mathcal{R}_n\) & Dirichlet Ring \(\mathcal{D}_f\): \[\mathcal{R}_n = K[x_1, \dots, x_n]/(x_1^2, \dots, x_n^2), \qquad \mathcal{D}_f(s) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{f(n)}{n^s}, \qquad (\vartheta_{\Omega} f)(n) = \Omega(n) f(n).\]

The 16 Global Algebraic Failure Modes (#1–#16)

Every algorithm contract in the 24-chapter FPS textbook cross-references these canonical failure modes:

Failure Mode Hazard Name & Algebraic Trigger Exact Defensive Remedy & Target Chapter
Failure Mode #1 Precision Loss in Shifted Division \(B(x)/A(x) \bmod x^N\) when \(\nu(A) = v \ge 1\) Dividing by \(x^v\) shifts degree \(N+v-1\) down to \(N-1\); always compute both \(A(x)\) and \(B(x)\) to precision \(\bmod x^{N+v}\) before shifting (Ch 01.4).
Failure Mode #2 3-Way Modular Reduction Trap in \(P(x)^\alpha \bmod x^N\) when \(\alpha \ge p\) (1) Shift \(v \cdot \alpha\) in true integer __int128 (return \(0\) if \(v > 0\) and \(v\alpha \ge N\)); (2) leading scalar \(c_v^{\alpha \bmod (p-1)}\) by Fermat; (3) series exponent \((\alpha \bmod p)\ln \widetilde{P}\) (Ch 02.3).
Failure Mode #3 Rational Exponent \(P(x)^{a/b}\) Existence Failure (\(\gcd(a,b)=1\)) \(P(x)^{a/b} \in \mathbb{F}_p[[x]]\) exists iff \(b \mid (v \cdot a)\) in \(\mathbb{Z}\) and the leading coefficient \(c_v\) is a \(b\)-th residue in \(\mathbb{F}_p^\times\) (Ch 02.3).
Failure Mode #4 Characteristic-\(p\) Transcendental Breakdown (\(\ln, \exp, \int\)) when \(p \le N\) \(\int x^{p-1}dx = x^p/p\) divides by \(0 \bmod p\); replace \(\ln/\exp\) with algebraic inv, Bostan–Mori, Frobenius \(A(x)^p \equiv A(x^p) \pmod p\), or Boxed Products (Ch 02.2, Ch 04.3).
Failure Mode #5 Lagrange Inversion \(1/n\) Breakdown when \(p \mid n\) Classical LIF \(\frac{1}{n}[u^{n-1}]H'(u)\Phi(u)^n\) divides by \(n \equiv 0 \pmod p\); always use Derivative-Free Residue LIF (Form 2): \([u^n]H(u)(\Phi(u) - u\Phi'(u))\Phi(u)^{n-1}\) (Ch 11.2).
Failure Mode #6 Compositional Inverse (revert) Breakdown when \(p \le N\) Route A (noshi91 Power Projection + pow) divides by \(1, \dots, N-1\); switch to Route B (Newton Iteration + Kinoshita–Li compose), which divides only by \(2\) and \(f_1\) (Ch 21.4).
Failure Mode #7 2D Obstinate Kernel Over-Subtraction & Newton–Girard Symmetric Function Asymmetry (\(p_k \leftrightarrow e_k\)) when \(p \le N\) Subtracting \(F(x,0)+F(0,y)\) double-counts \(F(0,0)\) (\(\to +F(0,0)\)); for \(E(t) = \sum_{k \ge 0} e_k t^k\), \(e_k \to p_k\) via \(P(t) = -t E'(-t)/E(-t)\) uses only inv (\(e_0=1\)) and is 100% division-free in all characteristics \(p\); \(p_k \to e_k\) divides by \(k\) and fails for \(p \le N\) (Ch 17.3).
Failure Mode #8 Vanishing Indicial Polynomial \(I(n^*) \equiv 0 \pmod p\) in P-Recursive Unrolling Supply initial value if \(I(n^*) = 0\) in \(\mathbb{Z}\); lift the recurrence to the \(p\)-adic quotient ring \(\mathbb{Z}/p^{V+1}\mathbb{Z}\) (\(u \cdot p^e\)) where \(V = \sum_{I(n)\equiv 0} v_p(I(n))\) (Ch 18.4).
Failure Mode #9 Gaussian \(q\)-Binomial Pole when \(\operatorname{ord}_p(q) = d \le n\) Direct \((q;q)_n\) division hits \(1 - q^d \equiv 0 \pmod p\); apply the \(q\)-Lucas Theorem \(\begin{bmatrix} n_1 d + n_0 \\ k_1 d + k_0 \end{bmatrix}_q \equiv \binom{n_1}{k_1}\begin{bmatrix} n_0 \\ k_0 \end{bmatrix}_q \pmod p\) (Ch 23.2).
Failure Mode #10 Dynamic Division by Zero in Point-Value / Tree-DP Products When a light child evaluates to \(F_v(\omega_r) \equiv 0 \pmod p\), maintain each frequency state in the Zero-Divisor Monoid \((a, c) \equiv a \cdot 0^c\) (\(a \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times, c \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\)) (Ch 22.3).
Failure Mode #11 Missing \(m\)-th Root of Unity in Filter (单位根反演) when \(m \nmid (p-1)\) Do not search for a scalar \(\omega_m \in \mathbb{F}_p\); compute modulo the Cyclotomic Polynomial \(\Phi_m(\omega)\) in \(\mathbb{F}_p[\omega]/(\Phi_m(\omega))\) (Ch 07.4).
Failure Mode #12 Bluestein Chirp-Z Square-Root Failure (\(c^{1/2} \notin \mathbb{F}_p\)) & \(c=0\) Trap Never use \(ij = \frac{1}{2}((i+j)^2 - i^2 - j^2)\); always use Bluestein’s Binomial Identity \(ij = \binom{i+j}{2} - \binom{i}{2} - \binom{j}{2}\), and branch on \(c = 0\) (Ch 07.3).
Failure Mode #13 Full-Online Self-Convolution Asymmetry Bug at \(l > 0\) In cdq(l, r) for \(f_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} f_i f_{n-i}\), convolve \(f[0..m]^2\) at \(l=0\), but convolve \(2 \cdot f[l..m] \times f[0..r-l]\) at \(l > 0\) (Ch 09.2).
Failure Mode #14 Negative-Integer \(\alpha = -d\) Pole in Generalized \(\alpha\)-Euler Transform Split \(F(y) = F_{\le d}(y) + y^{d+1} F_{>d}(y)\) before applying \((1-x)^{-\alpha}\) so the tail shifts \(\alpha \mapsto 1 > 0\) (Ch 06.4).
Failure Mode #15 Hidden Pole at \(z = 0\) in Improper Rational Diagonals (\(\deg_y A \ge \deg_y B\)) Substituting \(y = t/z\) introduces \(z^{\deg_y B - \deg_y A - 1}\); include \(\operatorname{Res}_{z=0}\) or perform polynomial division in \(y\) first (Ch 16.4).
Failure Mode #16 Catalan Negative Upper-Index Trap (\(k=0\)) & Prüfer \(N=1\) Trap Isolate \(k=0\) before \(\binom{2k-1}{k} = \binom{2k-1}{k-1}\) (\(\binom{-1}{0}=1 \neq \binom{-1}{-1}=0\)), and handle \(N=1\) before \((N-2)!\) Prüfer extraction (Ch 04.4, Ch 11.3).
