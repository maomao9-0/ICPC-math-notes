FPS Study Plan & Diagnostic Mastery Gates

Learning path, blind checkpoint protocol, and 24-chapter mastery table

This study plan guides you through the 6-Part, 24-Chapter Formal Power Series (FPS) & Generating Functions Textbook (Ch 01–Ch 24), its 6 Two-Stage Blind Topic-Group Checkpoints (CP-I–CP-VI), and the 14-Problem Multi-Perspective Synthesis Reserve (Syn-01–Syn-14).

The progression is zero-forward-reference: starting from the formal power series ring \(K[[x]]\) and valuation topology in Ch 01, every chapter relies only on earlier chapters in the dependency graph. Keep the FPS Notation, Operators & 16 Failure Modes open as you work.

Weekly Study Rhythm & Blind Checkpoint Protocol

  • Session A — Theory & Scratchpad (90 min): Reproduce the chapter’s central algebraic derivations on paper and verify the small hand-worked examples through degree \(3\) or \(4\). Do not move to problems until you can state why each division is valid and which of the 16 Global Failure Modes applies.
  • Session B — Stepping Stones & Intermediate Ladder (2 h): Solve SS-1, SS-2, Int-1, and Int-2 from the chapter’s 8-problem ladder. Record which statement clue triggered the generating function model.
  • Session C — Advanced & Mastery Ladder (2.5–3 h): Attempt Adv-1, Adv-2, Mas-1, and Mas-2. Write down the target complexity in \(\mathcal{M}(N)\) and verify characteristic-\(p\) and valuation-shift preconditions before coding.
  • Part Gate — Two-Stage Blind Checkpoint (CP-I–CP-VI): At the end of each Part, attempt the 8 blind checkpoint problems without topic labels:
    1. Stage 1 (10 minutes per problem, no hints): Classify the algebraic object (OGF vs. EGF vs. PGF vs. Laurent residue vs. bivariate diagonal vs. SPS) and write the target functional equation.
    2. Stage 2 (Full solve): Solve at least 6 of 8 blind problems before advancing to the next Part.
